# Existence Is Finite

**Question.** Which conditions force a form to have one recoverable finite construction, and how much of its medium do those conditions determine? The ontological proposal is that every actual form has such a construction. To establish it, determinacy must supply finite generation and recoverable constructor roles, rather than borrow them from the chosen notation.

**Result.** Finite generation with recoverable operands and a separate constructor origin gives unique admitted tree representations (EF8). It leaves the admitted construction language open. Requiring every ordered pair to be constructible selects the full finite-tree medium, whose carrier is infinite even though each member is finite (EF5). These are distinct conclusions. Applying them to the closed whole requires establishing the corresponding relations there.

**EF1 (Finite description versus finite object).** C6 motivates a finitist interpretation. It does not prove that every finitely specified object has finitely many constituents. For example, the rule naming all natural numbers is finite, while its extension in ordinary mathematics is infinite. Restricting actuality to finite generated objects is a substantive condition here. Every finite stage of a history can satisfy it without supplying a fixed bound on all stages or making their totality one finite tree.

**EF2 (Construction must retain its inputs).** To recover a pair's two arguments, pairing must be injective. To recover whether construction occurred, its image must exclude the floor. These are the two constructor conditions in Definition 1. They are structural preservation requirements. In this grammar even equal children form a node distinct from the floor: collapsing every pair `(a,a)` to the floor would violate EF2. They do not follow just from having definite elements or a binary operation.

**EF3 (Countermodel to generation alone).** Take the two-element carrier `{0,1}`, floor 0, and pairing `p(0,0)=1` with all other pairs mapped to 0. Both elements have finite generating terms. Equality is definite and decidable. Yet `p(p(0,0),0)=0`, so distinct finite trees denote the same element. The unique evaluation from syntax is onto and fails to be injective. This countermodel refutes the earlier step from generation and determinacy to unique tree representation.

**EF4 (The Swift floor has two roles).** Mathematical tree syntax distinguishes a leaf from every constructed pair. The Swift `Null` protocol instead certifies equality of two associated types. Many nominal types can conform to it without being `Never`. Thus `Null` as an equality witness and the concrete syntax leaf must not be identified. `Checks/ClosedInformation.swift` accepts an equality witness with two `Never` sides and separately rejects its proposed identity with `Never`. The open protocol lattice needs a specified finite constructor interpretation before Theorem 1 applies to it.

**EF5 (A free constructor medium cannot have a finite carrier).** If M has a floor and total pairing satisfying EF2, the disjoint constructors give an injection `1 + M² → M`. A finite cardinal m would therefore satisfy `1 + m² ≤ m`, which fails for every nonnegative integer m. Equivalently, start at t₀=⊥ and set t_(n+1)=⟨t_n,⊥⟩. All these elements are distinct: an alleged equality can be shortened by constructor injectivity until a pair equals the floor. Every t_n is finite, while their carrier is infinite. In ordinary set semantics Σ is countably infinite, since finite constructor strings enumerate it and this chain supplies infinitely many elements.

Interference Is Substitution IiS17 applies this countability boundary to exact channel descriptions: a fixed-size matrix can contain unrestricted scalar values that no finite constructor string names. A finite symbolic family and an identified member of that family are different records.

**EF6 (A finite search requires a uniform bound).** Let Σ_d contain the unlabeled ordered binary trees of height at most d, with leaf height zero. Then `|Σ₀|=1` and `|Σ_(d+1)|=1+|Σ_d|²`. The first cardinalities are 1, 2, 5, 26, 677. Each Σ_d is finite and their union is Σ. Pairing maps `Σ_d × Σ_d` into `Σ_(d+1)`, not generally back into Σ_d. A finite search may restrict constructor use to keep constructions within its declared bound. It cannot retain total free pairing on that same finite carrier.

The quantifiers matter: `for every t there is a finite height d(t)` does not give `one d that bounds every candidate`. A finite target has some height, but a search procedure must be supplied a sufficient bound or a separate way to obtain one. Reading a finitely presented target may provide it. Knowing only that an unknown target is finite does not. Decidable equality permits comparing any two supplied trees without making an unrestricted enumeration of all trees a terminating negative search.

VC23 separates another pair of quantifiers. For countably many finite-valued addresses with admission specified entirely by finite-address relations, an impossible joint assignment has a finite inconsistent subfamily. Their sizes can still be unbounded across problems. A carrier of finite generated objects need not satisfy that premise: a natural number larger than each stated n exists for every finite list of bounds, while no natural number exceeds all of them. A finite argument using the universal requirement proves impossibility, although no finite list of those individual bounds does. Finite ground evidence and finite generic proof are different resources. The numeral carrier here is fixed to the finite grammar; admitting a new infinite value would change it.

Matter M69 gives a constructive use of this distinction. In its declared balanced graph family, a requested residual bridge with a finite side has a finite cut witness even when the graph is unbounded and other directions remain unresolved. The argument uses finitely many actual incidences for that question, rather than deriving a finite whole from C6. Finite support of a comparison, finite representation of each witness and a uniform bound on all witnesses are different requirements.

**EF7 (Finite levels do not imply a finite tower).** The sequence of levels `L_n={t_n}` contains one finite candidate at each level and admits another level for every n. Likewise, repeatedly taking a finite power set yields a finite next level without producing a last one. Termination follows instead if every continued level strictly decreases a natural-number rank supplied at the start. A starting rank r allows at most r descents, by induction on r. Non-increase alone permits an endless repeated rank. This is the missing height premise in Verification Is Identification §5.16.

**EF8 (Unique decomposition with partial construction).** Let M have a specified floor ⊥ and an ordered constructor p defined on D⊆M×M. Suppose every element has a finite generating tree of admitted applications. Suppose two single-valued readings recover the operands on the constructor range: l(p(a,b))=a and r(p(a,b))=b. Suppose an origin reading distinguishes ⊥ from every constructed output. Then each element of M has exactly one admitted finite constructor tree. Total pairing is not needed for this uniqueness.

*Proof.* Equal constructed outputs give equal left and right operands by applying l and r. Thus p is injective on D. The origin reading makes its range disjoint from the floor. Finite generation gives a representing tree for each element. If two such trees have the same evaluation, either both are leaves or both are nodes. A leaf and a node cannot agree by the origin distinction. In the node case, recovery equates the two pairs of evaluated children. Induction on the total size of the compared finite trees identifies both child trees and hence the original trees. ∎

Let L be the admitted finite-tree language of this partial algebra. It contains the leaf and contains every subtree of each of its trees. Evaluation is a bijection L→M by the preceding proof. It preserves and reflects constructor availability: p(a,b) is defined exactly when the node joining the unique trees for a and b belongs to L. Conversely, any finite-tree language containing the leaf and closed under taking subtrees defines such a partial algebra by admitting exactly its own nodes. Therefore fixing L determines one partial constructor medium up to the unique isomorphism preserving the floor, constructor and its domain. Unique decomposition leaves admissibility as a further coordinate, rather than silently making every pair available.

For example, let t₀=⊥ and tₙ₊₁=⟨tₙ,tₙ⟩. The language {t₀,t₁,…} admits only pairing equal members. Each member has a unique finite decomposition, but ⟨t₀,t₁⟩ is not admitted. The finite truncation {t₀,…,t_d} is another such language, admitting its diagonal pairs only below the height bound. These examples differ from the unrestricted finite-tree medium. Adding total pairing to EF8 forces L to contain every finite tree by induction and recovers Theorem 1.

This settles one global uniqueness question negatively under precise premises. Even finite generation, recoverable operands and a distinct origin permit inequivalent whole construction languages: the diagonal language and the full tree language disagree on whether the same two admitted operands can combine. No isomorphism preserving constructor availability can identify them. Their constructions use only forms inside the respective medium. To exclude one requires an additional internal admission relation, not another proof of unique decomposition. A question can nevertheless have a forced answer across both, as the following corollary shows.

**Corollary (structural identity is invariant on shared admitted descriptions).** Let M and N satisfy EF8, possibly with different admitted languages. For finite trees s and t admitted by both, eval_M(s)=eval_M(t) exactly when s=t, exactly when eval_N(s)=eval_N(t). Each evaluation is injective by EF8. Thus this equality question has the same answer in every such realization admitting the two descriptions, without fixing the entire construction language or medium in advance. This concerns the interpreted constructor identity, not every other question that can be asked about a source or its history.

The origin reading is a premise about the represented construction, not a label capable of repairing an actual merger. If a putative node already equals the floor, no single-valued reading of that output can return distinct origins. The floor is a terminal in this grammar, not a claim that the whole reality once had no relations. Recovering ordered operands also names their roles. Keeping an unordered collection alone does not recover an ordered pair.

**EF9 (Full retention does not make every local result a free constructor).** A construction can retain its context and both operands while projecting them to a noninjective local result. It can also restrict which operand pairs it admits. Thus full retention alone does not establish either local constructor recovery or total constructor availability.

*Proof.* Write the prepared source as (h,(a,b)) and its complete retained result as ((h,(a,b)),a). The complete result recovers h,a,b. Its local result a identifies (a,b₀) with (a,b₁) whenever b₀≠b₁. The lost local distinction remains at its retained address. For the second assertion, admit only a=b and return the local node ⟨a,a⟩, retaining the entire prepared source in the same way. Every admitted construction retains its inputs, yet a pair with unequal operands is outside its domain. ∎

`RetainedConstruction` uses the existing `RetainedWhole` with context and operands in its actual input. `ConstructionRecoversOperands` derives the operand reading from `Permanent` and that address. `SameBoundaryForcesOperands` permits different contexts and local results while deriving the same operand answer. `RetainedOutputIdentifiesSource` derives input identity from an equality of complete retained outputs. None adds its concluding equality as a caller's premise. `DiagonalConstruction` computes its local pair from its one operand parameter and admits no independent second operand.

The open `Permanent` equations alone do not establish fresh construction or an origin distinction: the assignment Left=Right=Never satisfies them. Explicit `RetainedWhole` outputs use the nominal Paired constructor, which is distinct from the Never leaf in the stated syntax. Repeated equal local results can therefore belong to distinct retained occurrences. Source recovery, fresh occurrence and finite generation remain separately addressed relations. A complete construction record can establish the first two in this grammar without granting total availability of every proposed interaction.

**EF10 (A recursive axis, a settled output and a decision are different obligations).** IntegerValued requires only that Right conform to IntegerValued again. It specifies neither a terminal nor a unit on Left. The existing CyclicNumeral in Checks/FiniteBoundary.swift has Right equal to itself. Finite expressions in a declared Never/Succ grammar admit a natural-number interpretation by induction, and the existing SpendSuccessor constructor exposes the actual predecessor. This interpretation does not extend automatically to every conformer. The numeral Zero and the type Never can both serve as zero under different stated conventions without being the same nominal type. The runtime Structure.count reader is not the definition of this unary interpretation.

A recursive constraint can nevertheless establish an exclusion without certifying termination. `EmptyCandidateSupport` now binds the node's original `CandidateNode.Leaf` to Never and requires the same property of both children. No finite child path can then reach a genuine `CandidateLeaf`, whose original conformance binds Leaf to itself. An empty cycle satisfies that condition without a terminal. Conversely a leaf with an empty payload remains a leaf. Verdict VC31 records the formerly accepted false allocation and the compiler refusals that now preserve this distinction. Finite syntax, finite depth and absence of a reachable leaf have different witnesses.

The general exclusion has an exact relation behind it. Fix nodes C, an actual child relation R and a specified forbidden set D. Let Reach(D) contain the nodes with a finite R-path, including a zero-length path, to D. Define K(S)={x∈C∖D: every R-child of x belongs to S}. Then C∖Reach(D) is the greatest set E satisfying E⊆K(E), and is itself fixed by K. To prove the first statement, any finite path from a member of E remains in E by induction and cannot end in D. Conversely, a node with no path to D is outside D and each child has no path to D. If a node is outside D and all its children have that property, it too has no such path. These two directions give the fixed equation. Neither a finite carrier nor finite unfolding of its members is required.

This explains why the empty-support cycle is a valid exclusion certificate. Its original node tags exclude a leaf at the root, and its complete child obligations preserve that fact along every finite path. A declaration only saying that another node certifies it would omit both checks. A positive claim that a path reaches D instead requires the endpoint relation and a path to it. Substituting self-supporting claims for that path changes the question. This does not make every positive theorem inductive or every negative theorem coinductive: polarity of the sentence is not the criterion. The interpreted relation and the claimed consequence determine what closes the proof. Verdict VC23 separates finite proof generation, supported circular assignments and consequences forced by an actual given within those assignments.

Measurable requires a Null output, meaning equality of that output's two sides. It says nothing relating two distinct measurement declarations that share an input. Checks/ClosedInformation.swift constructs two Measurable coordinates with the same input and different output-side values, Never and Lit1. Each output is an actual IdentityReading, whose existing source/answer equations now also derive its Null conformance. The selected value remains a parameter of each complete declaration. The declarations differ even though their Left projections agree: this does not exhibit two outcomes from an identical complete physical state or remove information from the whole. It exhibits an unbound reading-law coordinate. A settled result can carry a value, but its settlement alone does not establish a common measurement law.

Decidable requires a Pair output. A tuple with both fields Lit2 satisfies this shape just as a tuple with Never fields does. The constraint defines neither an exclusive two-verdict alphabet nor a terminating procedure that selects a verdict from the input. These are separate obligations of the actual decision construction. Failure to establish a conformance is likewise not an encoded negative verdict.

The constructive replacements already exist for specific questions. PredicateReading names an application, while concrete projection constructors and ReadingFactors bind its actual source and result. SharedDecoder derives agreement through the same decoder application (Verdict VC10). G2f derives the zero relation of a generated tree difference through recursive constraints and source equalities. Those constructions establish their stated relations without a measurement runtime. A complete binary comparison additionally needs a positive nonzero witness and coverage of the declared finite domain. The marker names alone supply neither.

## Part I — From determinacy to a proposed construction grammar

Start with the encountered content and ask which relations must remain for that content, or a specified act concerning it, to remain. A necessary condition, a sufficient construction and a chosen representation grammar have different roles in this inquiry.

**C1 — Something is.** An occurring denial cannot cancel its own occurrence. The Cut's Step 1 starts with this given. Step 2 separately points to an encountered contrast, further content of that same actuality. It is marked as given, not derived from nonemptiness. An account that removes that contrast fails to preserve the exhibited datum. An arbitrary pair of names, or a contrast with a hypothetical absence, does not by itself establish two distinct actual contents.

The refusal has an exact source scope. A description claiming that the actuality containing its own occurring assertion is wholly empty fails to represent that actuality. A description concerning an absent object need not itself be absent. Its occurrence establishes neither the existence nor the impossibility of its stated referent. This is the report/source distinction below applied to the attempted empty account, not a derivation of permanence, internal cycles or the necessity of happening from the syntax of a denial. The Cut §5 separates these claims before introducing the record commitment.

**C2 — Determinacy has a relational meaning.** The Cut's Step 3 adopts the rule that a proposed actual distinction must make a difference to some internal relation. A description that excludes every such difference adds no further state under that rule. This is the book's marked jurisdiction, not a consequence of nonemptiness alone. It also does not require every question about a determinate form to be settled already.

**C3 — A settled common answer preserves agreement.** If two readings of the same stated question must equal one common answer, they must equal each other. Positively different readings exclude that common-answer construction. Both records and their disagreement can still belong to one determinate whole. This is the scoped settlement condition of The Cut §18 and Verdict VC60, rather than a proof that every context has a single Boolean valuation or that storing a contradiction entails every statement.

**C4 — A complement in a description supplies no outside substance.** Under C2, an additional substrate contributes only where it introduces a distinguishable relation. Otherwise it adds a name without another admitted distinction. Writing "not this" marks what a description excludes; it does not require a second existing object outside the whole. Nor does identifying a relation establish two independently combinable parts. LQ15–LQ16 state the additional conditions for independent blocks and reconstructible contact roles.

The two compared roles of a contrast also do not determine an exhaustive binary partition. Fix a≠b in a described carrier X and require a total binary reading s with s(a)=0 and s(b)=1. Such a reading partitions X, but the contrast alone does not assign any further c to either class. On X={a,b,c}, both assignments to c retain the same contrast. In general the set s⁻¹(0) can be any subset containing a and excluding b until further relations constrain it. If the carrier is already established to contain exactly a and b, the partition is determined. The exhaustion of that carrier, as well as the demand for a total binary reading, is additional content. This is an insufficiency test for the description, not an assertion that the actual whole has a selectable third form or Boolean state space.

Calling the distinguishing relation a boundary does not yet establish spatial adjacency or transmission. The admitted forms and their retained contrasts can be kept fixed while the proposed rearrangement relation changes, as LQ12 proves. In particular, identity-only rearrangement and a larger admitted rearrangement relation can preserve the same contrast data. Hence those data alone do not force a nontrivial transmission step or a direction of influence. This does not remove the actual relation carrying the given contrast. It distinguishes that relation from an additional claim about what can be changed or sent through it. The Cut's early point of contact has this relational meaning. A physical surface, causal channel or newly born participant requires its own established correspondence.

### The graph of requirements behind these steps

Take the specific datum G: a contrast between contents a and b is jointly experienced at an occurrence g. The contents here are those encountered, without yet identifying their physical sources. G includes occurrence, experiencing, actual contrast and its joint presentation at g. These are aspects extracted from the datum. Calling experiencing qualia introduces no additional participant. Under The Cut's registration rule, calling the carrying relation a witness also does not require another object. None of these unpackings has yet derived the organization that makes experiencing possible.

The common address matters. From a jointly experienced contrast one can extract that experiencing occurs and that a contrast occurs. The two separate existential claims do not recover their original connection. For an incidence test, take distinct occurrence addresses g,h and distinct contents a,b. A record family containing only (g,a,a) and (h,b,b) exposes a and b in its separate endpoint projections. It contains no occurrence with the listed pair (a,b). Combining those projections would invent the missing joint incidence. This is a counterdescription to that inference, without a claim about which phenomenal arrangements are physically possible. The address g names the given occurrence, not a pre-existing subject, maximal region or container whose unity has been assumed.

The recovery row has a precise form that permits multivalued contacts. Let T relate possible sources x to complete available reading inputs m, including any context used by the reading. Let q be the stipulated settled question. An exact decoder exists on the admitted image precisely when

    T(x,m) and T(y,m) imply q(x)=q(y).

Necessity follows because both answers must equal the decoder's answer at m. For sufficiency, assign to each admitted m the unique q-answer shared by its related sources. This defines a mathematical reading without choosing a source representative. It supplies neither an instrument implementing that reading nor the actuality of a proposed T. In particular, one source may have several contact results. This is the existing factorization criterion of Verdict §6c applied to the two projections of the contact. `ReadingFactors` expresses particular source-bound recoveries in the type layer. For a two-answer contrast the criterion says that the two answer classes have disjoint sets of complete reading inputs. Distinction can reside in joint context rather than in a separate mark or copy.

The graph therefore needs joint premises. Actual contrast alone does not require its later recovery. The demand for exact recovery alone does not establish that it is possible. Their combination constrains every realization meeting that demand. Nor does equality of the resulting answer identify the original occurrences, as C3 and VC60 distinguish. When two descriptions imply the same answers, one may merge their answer role for that question only after retaining whatever source correspondence the inquiry still needs.

An identification of internal participants must also be separated from one code for their whole joint form. M71 permits a singleton code for one known source when its fixed decoder retains that source's structure. It forbids erasing the internal contrast from the interpreted result. For varying sources, different required answers must remain different in the complete input comprising code, decoding configuration and context. Selecting a source-specific decoder puts the distinction there. A name for the one actual whole does not thereby supply knowledge of its detailed relations. The comparison family tests descriptions of that same actuality, rather than creating other actual worlds.

Retaining the occurrence address and its participating parts may still omit how those parts are joined. VI1 supplies an exact instance: several correspondences between the same two structures retain relative maps up to one common change of description. Their simultaneous conjugacy class is complete for that comparison. Taking a separate invariant of each relative map can lose whether two of them agree. Thus removing absolute labels preserves more than a list of separately reduced parts. The joint relation itself carries the distinction. `JointEvidenceFromMemory` constructs recovery of a specified pair of answers from one observation. Recovering a further question about their joining additionally requires that it factor through that pair. This uses VC9's existing criterion and supplies no extra physical witness by naming a relation.

VI1 and VI1a already give a sharper conjunction. On an unoriented two-role structure preserved by exchange, no deterministic equivariant reading can select one role: the exchange fixes the full input and fixes neither candidate. Yet the simultaneous exchange preserves whether two compared roles are the same or different. Collapsing both roles to their one unary orbit loses that contrast and fails M71. Thus the same relational input can forbid both selecting one participant and identifying the participants. Adding an orientation can enable selection. Erasing a given contrast changes the source instead. This is the existing symmetry example, not a new theorem or a claim that every experienced contrast has this symmetry. It shows why absence of an isolated distinguishing label cannot eliminate relational multiplicity or justify The Cut's stronger performer premise.

Retaining the joint requirement also differs from retaining only the content each side can recover separately. LQ13 and M72 exhibit a contact whose complete boundary includes a conjugation action, while its separately common content is a commutative quotient. Replacing the contact by equality in that quotient admits previously refused combinations. The original relation remains expressible through a joint equality carrying the omitted action. Thus the graph must retain how its roles constrain each other, including refusals, rather than only answers every role can repeat.

This requirement does not force a particular graph of physical mediation. LQ13 represents any finite-arity admission relation by binary incidences to one shared witness. Separate existential witnesses would lose the joint restriction. A shared context label also remains insufficient when its pairing fiber is not rectangular. The distinction is between a preserved joining and its erased projections, not between a particular number of drawn arrows or internal objects. The incidence witness can encode the original tuple without identifying its actual occurrence. Replicating all such witnesses leaves admission unchanged, while a question about their occurrence identity would change. No subject count or causal integration law has been derived from these representation choices.

The source correspondence also connects the self-reference and report branches. For an assertion to identify its own occurrence correctly, the occurrence it refers to must be that very occurrence of the assertion. This particular equality does not establish a model of every aspect of its source, a later return in time, or experiencing. A report of experiencing supplies a different direct route to occurrence: the report itself occurs. Its actuality alone does not establish its reported content. Conversely, omitting experiencing from a formal description does not prove that its realization lacks it. These distinctions apply equally to a report issued by the present analysis. No external level of certification has been introduced.

Complete recovery is also narrower than duplication or experienced unity. VC9's full decoder answers every specified question about its source, including questions about the actual source-image graph. Its two-state example separates that ability from performing the source's next step: the source exchanges its forms while the recorded image is unchanged. A dynamically matching image needs the additional transition equation. Neither correct source answers nor an isomorphism of specified structures identifies two occurrences. To ask whether they belong to one experienced occurrence, retain the joint relation of G instead of substituting a recovery law for it. This corrects The Cut §14's earlier conflation of full access and duplication without declaring shared feeling possible or impossible.

An invariant answer set and a retained individual answer also require different return relations. PP15 gives an exact admission-set example: exchange two distinct answers. Their set returns while neither answer does. A deterministic choice from that set which must respect the exchange would have to return the chosen answer itself, which is refused. Keeping two separate applications instead would supply two answer occurrences, without the identification the single-answer demand requires. Thus this branch needs the same source and occurrence discipline as joint givenness and recovery. The obstruction depends on that discipline, not on identifying every logical question with a Boolean reading.

Several tempting edges remain open. Jointly experienced contrast has not forced a separate subject, a self-model, a complete boundary of this occurrence, or an order of earlier and later. This does not establish their physical dispensability. It identifies obligations not discharged by the stated datum. A shared participating role does not identify two joint occurrences. More basically, a greatest region among all compatible participations and the boundary of this actual occurrence are different questions. Liquid LQ15 shows that every downward-closed admission family can arise by forgetting occurrence indices from individually specified complete sets. Failure of a greatest region in that projection therefore cannot refute unity of the original datum. If membership in one complete joint occurrence is already established, naming its fiber supplies no further discovery of completeness. The needed relation connects this given to its full joint extent without acquiring that extent by definition. Historical retention still requires The Cut's record commitment, and its proposed connection from self-certification to felt presence still uses BRIDGE_INT.

To test an edge, retain its particular starting content and attempt to remove the proposed requirement. A necessity proof must show that every such removal loses that content or conflicts with an established relation. One failed construction, or failure to invent an alternative, does not establish this. After deriving several requirements, test the reverse direction separately: their conjunction may still omit the relation that made them aspects of this one datum. A proof cycle between assertions cannot replace that relation. The analysis and its records also occur within the whole, so the occurrence of a checking assertion does not certify its claimed source by itself. This map organizes such tests, without claiming a complete inventory of the conditions of experience.

### Interiority, continuation and a proposed minimum

The Cut §10b already uses qualia for bare experiencing as well as discussing particular experienced contents. A proposed I beneath qualia therefore has two possible targets: a distinction from specific contents, or an additional condition beneath experiencing itself. The first can be investigated through continuation of one situated occurrence. The second needs a further given or necessary relation. Renaming bare presence does not establish another layer.

**Which continuity is claimed?** A present jointly experienced contrast supplies its present joining. Continued self-report, retained memory, faithful reconstruction, actual continuation and identity of the experiencing form are further relations. Their coincidence must be established. An act that tries to change perspective still occurs somewhere within the whole. That supplies no external operator, but does not show that every local boundary survives the act. Defining internal change to mean boundary-preserving change would put the desired invariant into the admitted-operation family.

M71 gives an exact test for one proposed persistence rule. Let C contain the addressed pairs that the rule demands receive the same identity, and let D contain pairs whose identities are positively distinguished. Such labels exist exactly when no D-pair is connected by a finite chain of C-pairs, traversed in either direction. Necessity is equality substitution along the chain. For sufficiency, assign each role its class under that generated equivalence. This supplies the least identification required by C and preserves D. It is a test of this partial identity proposal, without asserting that C is the physical or experienced continuation relation.

If one earlier form is C-related to two later forms while D distinguishes those later identities, the two-edge chain already refuses that proposal. Several memory-preserving successors are not by themselves such a D-pair: different records or situated occurrences need not have been established as different subjects. Conversely, causal connection alone should not silently be made C. Every connected component would then receive one identity, however its participating forms were experienced. The relation whose preservation matters must be acquired independently of the desired subject count.

This gives a focused version of the proposed invariant: identify which actually established joining relation can be transported through a change while retaining the experienced contrast and its occurrence correspondence. VC9 shows why recovery of a source does not make its record the same occurrence. VI1a shows why an unpointed symmetric description can omit the reference needed to select one participant. The given occurrence supplies a situated starting point, without creating a separate recipient inside it. None of these results establishes indefinite continuation of that occurrence or physical impossibility of changing an interior boundary.

**A minimum and a gradient ask different questions.** A smallest sufficient reading depends on the questions and operations it must preserve. An indivisible joint relation concerns the failure of a proposed decomposition. A smallest physical carrier and a minimum experienced unit require further correspondence. LQ15's parity example is indecomposable under its specified independent splices, while a bijective change to different coordinates presents its admitted states as a product. The original splices are not transported to independent changes of those new coordinates. Thus a claimed relational atom must include its contact structure, rather than rely on one convenient picture of its parts.

LQ17 tests the gradient proposal without assigning it a phenomenological meaning. If one ordered scale gives exact disjoint regions at each level, its partitions must be nested. Crossed regional readings refuse that representation, although their joint reading gives a common refinement. An infinite nested family can also lack any smallest region among its admitted levels. These are useful attacks on a proposed account of interiority. They do not derive the lived boundary by classifying a graph. The remaining bridge is which contact establishes that these distinctions are given together in this occurrence, and which change preserves that same joint relation.

### Interiority as given and as an experimental question

Where an experienced contrast is given, its occurrence constrains an account immediately. It need not first be inferred from a bodily signal. This does not make every interpretation of its cause, extent or persistence equally given. A repeatable report is another occurrence, with a relation to what it reports. Reproducibility concerns a relation across preparations and readings, rather than numerical identity of the repeated occurrences. Neither an experiential report nor another physical instrument gains universal accuracy from repeatability alone.

The present obligations are therefore different:

| Target | Present ground | Missing ground |
|---|---|---|
| This experienced contrast occurs | The addressed datum G | No inference from a separate detector is needed to start here |
| These contents exhaust its joint extent | G supplies their joining | Coverage of the proposed boundary |
| A later form continues this same interiority | Proposed continuation links can be tested by M71 | Acquisition of those links as experienced continuation |
| A structural condition detects experiencing in other forms | The Cut's BRIDGE_INT supplies a commitment | A necessary connection or calibrated evidence with a stated domain |

**A joining can have a product family of contents.** The existing `CrossedRegionReadings<A,B>` supplies four addressed pairs: AA, AB, BA and BB. All combinations occur. Reading each first field and each second field retains the same four addresses, and pairing those readings at each address reconstructs the source. Thus a product family of contents and exact preservation of their joining can coexist. Removing the correspondence between addresses changes that construction. Decomposition with its correspondence retained is not erasure of the joining. This family alone does not establish which changes a physical system can perform.

This gives a concrete limit to the search for an atom of interiority. Joint membership alone does not force failure of product decomposition. Statistical dependence would require an additional law of weights, which this construction does not supply. Conversely, a forbidden recombination has not established experiencing. The finite example proves these structural distinctions, without assigning experience to its pairs. A claim that experienced joining necessarily has stronger restrictions must derive that extra relation from the experiencing datum. It cannot obtain it from the word joint.

Defining a boundary through preserving changes needs equal care. LQ13 now gives two finite contacts with the same full simultaneous symmetry group and the same admitted contact between distinct forms, but different boundaries for replacement with the partner retained. Supplied local operations recover that interface only when they connect all forms with the same contact profile. The contact's two sides are still part of the starting data. Neither this least interface nor a class of reachable forms identifies the complete membership of an experienced occurrence. The useful next question is which established relation a proposed substitution fails to preserve, with the other participating relations retained.

VC9 locates the experimental obligation. A record E(x,c) and decoder d claim to answer a source question q(x,c), where c retains the preparation, task and reading context. Establishing q=d∘E on one calibration domain does not establish it after a change of context. This is the exact counterpart of an empirical calibration, whose errors and uncertainty must also be retained. Even a reversible change in the records does not establish preservation of the source question. Conversely, a change in a record may come from the reporting task while the targeted contrast persists. These are obligations on the full internal contact. They introduce no observer outside reality.

[Frässle et al. (2014)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6827584/) provide a concrete test. In binocular rivalry they compared active report with passive viewing, using eye movements and pupil size as perceptual indicators alongside fMRI. The indicators correlated with reports in the active condition. Rivalry-related frontal activity was reduced or absent during passive viewing, while occipital and parietal activity persisted. Reporting also affected rivalry dynamics. This challenges transport of the active-report neural pattern as an unchanged marker. It does not demonstrate the absence of every frontal contribution or identify the full boundary of an experience. The passive interpretation still depends on the indicators tracking perception in that changed context.

The [Cogitate experiment (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1) separately varied visible stimulus content and duration to test preregistered neural predictions of IIT and GNWT. Such predictions expose theories to experimental disagreement. Their target is narrower than a necessary and sufficient detector of experiencing in arbitrary forms. VI must state its own discriminating prediction before treating a fit to these observations as evidence for BRIDGE_INT. No raw data from either study have been reanalysed here.

**The restriction also applies to a proposed impossibility proof.** VC15 identifies forms only relative to its declared questions and continuations. Agreement on currently accessible reports does not establish agreement on every relation within the whole. Under C2, appending a supposedly real interiority flag that changes no internal relation cannot establish two different realities. Appending a different experienced relation supplies a difference that the smaller interface has omitted, but its compatibility with the proposed physical organization still needs an argument. Thus we have neither a universal interiority detector nor a proof that one is impossible. An absence of a present bridge is not an impossibility theorem.

The next useful test must name one proposed invariant of the given joining, one internal change that puts it at risk, and a prediction on which preservation and loss disagree. Its interpretation must retain both the source relation and the reading's calibration. For an exact refusal, an established outcome must contradict an alternative's admission rule. For statistical discrimination, different weights or dependencies can distinguish hypotheses even when they admit the same individual outcomes. Agreement of their entire joint observation laws under every allowed testing strategy would instead leave those tests unable to distinguish them. Equal single-trial marginals do not establish that agreement. AC7 already supplies the statistical distinction. More region labels or stronger names for the same records do not supply the missing prediction.

### A proposed constructor grammar

The centre inquiry now has an explicit dependency map in Space §5i. Exchanging roles in one contact gives an action on participant readings only when those readings determine their partners. Unique centres require another relation, and S32 shows that coherent exchanges still do not force medial scaling. Under the stronger division laws, S30 reconstructs composition. A reading that exactly preserves every reflection and doubling can nevertheless lose halving and unique centres: S32 identifies the omitted distinctions at each depth. S31 separately asks what participation and internal detail future combinations need. These results distinguish a shared role, a reversible centre, a calibrated midpoint and a terminal of the constructor grammar. No one of these names supplies the others or establishes universal physical recovery.

**C5 — A proposed binary constructor representation.** Write a constructor form as ⟨a,b⟩ and its terminal as ⊥. In this representation each object is a terminal or a constructed pair. A finite division into more parts can be encoded with further binary divisions, provided the encoding retains the original roles and grouping. This does not identify a joint relation with its separate pair projections; VC23a supplies that obstruction. Equal children still form a node: ⟨a,a⟩ does not become ⊥ by the equality of its sides. Equality of sides and termination of construction are different relations (EF2, EF4). Labels and values must themselves receive representations if this is to be the complete constructor medium. EF8 identifies the recovery, origin and generation conditions under which such a representation is unique, including when construction is partial.

Liquid LQ16 supplies a separate route to recovering two roles: exact self-contact and contextual substitution reconstruct them from a ternary relation and force each admitted mixed result to be unique. Its two input places remain specified. Moreover, recombining an existing form with itself returns that form, whereas the free constructor here creates a distinct node. A rule for replacing fields and a rule for constructing a new retained form cannot be identified on the basis of their two argument places alone.

**C6 — Finite generation is a further condition.** The finitist proposal admits as individual forms only those generated through finitely many constructor applications. This supplies an induction principle on each form and permits structural comparison to terminate. It does not give one bound on every form or every inquiry (EF6–EF7).

An unfinished enumeration does not prove that its subject is indeterminate. In the mathematical sequence presentation, the condition xᵢ=0 at every natural-number address fixes one infinite sequence. Every coordinate answer is settled by one relation, without listing every address. This is a counterexample to the proposed inference from determinate specification to a finite represented object. It does not establish that such a presentation is physically realized. A finitist ontology must justify its further restriction directly.

Within that proposed restriction, infinity denotes unbounded further construction rather than a completed individual. A finite rule and its unbounded family of forms then have different roles. Liquid LQ15 locates a second distinction: all finite assemblies can be admitted while an infinite assembly is excluded. Finite-character admission permits that assembly when each finite restriction fits; the finite-support counterexample refuses it. Internal closure alone selects neither of these admission contracts.

Part II therefore states finite generation, total pairing and constructor recovery explicitly. Its uniqueness result answers what those conditions force. EF8 answers the more general question when pairing is partial.

## Part II — The one space, and the identity

**Definition 1.** A *medium* is an algebra for the functor F(X) = 1 + X²: a carrier M with a chosen element ⊥ and a pairing ⟨·,·⟩ : M × M → M, in which every element has a finite generating term. Require additionally that pairing is injective and that ⊥ is outside its image (EF2). These conditions specify a free constructor medium. They are not established for arbitrary physical configurations by C1–C6.

For the standard syntax-to-algebra construction, see [Niccolò Veltri, finite trees as an initial algebra](https://theorylunch.wordpress.com/2013/02/28/finite-trees-as-initial-algebra/).

**Theorem 1 — One space.** Up to a single isomorphism there is one medium: the initial F-algebra
> Σ = μX. (1 + X²),
the finite binary trees, with ⊥ the leaf and ⟨·,·⟩ the node.

*Proof.* Evaluate each finite tree in M to obtain the unique homomorphism h from the term algebra Σ. Finite generation makes h onto. To prove it injective, suppose h(s)=h(t). A leaf cannot equal a pair because the constructor images are disjoint. For two pairs, injectivity of pairing equates the two corresponding evaluated children. Induction on the finite trees gives equality of the children and then s=t. Thus h is an isomorphism, unique because every homomorphism must evaluate each term this way. ∎

The uniqueness is among media satisfying Definition 1. EF3 shows why an arbitrary finitely generated algebra can instead be a quotient of Σ. A quotient forgets syntactic distinctions according to its evaluation. The physical preservation question is whether the complete whole carries those distinctions elsewhere, as in Permanence Is Resistance M29–M35.

**The least fixpoint.** In the mathematical interpretation, μ means the carrier consists of terms built by finitely many constructor applications. Every further finite term is already a member of Σ, while an infinite tree is not. The structure map `1 + Σ² ≅ Σ` expresses closure under the disjoint constructors. EF5 shows that this carrier is infinite despite each member being finite. Interpreting the grammar as potential rather than completed infinity is a separate ontological position. The initial-algebra theorem does not turn the collection of all finite terms into one finite term.

**Theorem 2 — Conditional representation.** Every element of a medium satisfying Definition 1 has a unique finite-tree representation. Applying this to every existent additionally requires the ontological thesis and EF2 for the proposed whole.

*Proof.* Apply the inverse of the isomorphism in Theorem 1. ∎

The mathematical result identifies the specified medium with finite-tree syntax. Whether all physical existence has this form remains the theory's foundational question.

**Theorem 3 — Comparison is decidable.** There is a total decidable map COMPARE : Σ × Σ → {equal, unequal} with COMPARE(s, t) = equal exactly when s = t.

*Proof.* Recur on structure: COMPARE(⊥, ⊥) = equal; COMPARE(⟨a,b⟩, ⟨c,d⟩) = equal when COMPARE(a,c) and COMPARE(b,d) are both equal; otherwise unequal. Each call descends into a finite tree under Definition 1's generation premise, so each ends. Induction on the compared trees gives correctness. ∎

Each supplied pair of trees has a terminating comparison. This is a decidability result, not a uniform bound on all input sizes or all searches.

**Corollary — Verification, and its reach.** Take a finite F ⊆ Σ and a target τ, and let S = { y ∈ F : COMPARE(y, τ) = equal }. Then S is computable, and if |S| = 1, say S = {σ}, then for every y in F the verdict "y passes" and the identity "y is σ" are the same. To verify the lone survivor is to identify it. This is the verification-is-identification result, with its theory on separate ground. The claim applies wherever the finite-tree representation is faithful. A finite syntax for a semantic question does not itself make that question decidable.

## Part III — Reading, and scope

**A space is not a process.** With a fixed finite F and target τ, exhaustive structural comparison gives the same survivor set in every traversal order. This does not make traversal order the only freedom of an agent or of the whole. Changing the inputs, admission relation, available operations or stopping criterion changes the question. M67 separately derives permitted event orders from a specified prerequisite structure; LQ16 derives replacement roles from a specified contact relation. Neither structure is supplied by the existence of the tree space alone.

Theorem 3 decides structural equality for finite trees. It does not decide every predicate expressible about trees. A Swift conformance checks the constraints actually declared. Open recursive protocols need not enforce finite well-foundedness, as the cycle accepted in `Checks/FiniteBoundary.swift` demonstrates. A green build alone does not discharge the representation and finiteness premises of this paper.

**One whole, different mathematical roles.** A complete current configuration ω, its internal components, a carrier K of possible complete configurations, and the syntax Σ used to describe them are different objects in the model. Describing alternatives does not posit an external reservoir or additional actual worlds. Dynamical closure `U:K→K` says that evolution maps K into K. It does not require all formal pairings to be physically admissible transformations of the current whole. If Ω is modeled as one finite tree, its finite set of subtrees cannot also be a medium closed under total free pairing, by EF5. If Ω instead names the entire mathematical carrier Σ, that carrier is not finite. Neither identification follows just from the claim that there is literally nothing outside the whole.

The choice is consequential. A fixed finite K with preserved distinctions has the recurrence limit of Permanence Is Resistance M33 and Verdict VC34. An unbounded family of finite histories can grow without a fixed capacity bound. Both have finite configurations at each finite stage, but they support different statements about permanent discovery.

`SpendSuccessor` supplies a generic step from `Succ<P>` to P. The stopping argument applies to the finite grammar built from `Never` and `Succ`, not to every open `IntegerValued` conformance. The predecessor relation excludes an unchanged budget and a successor step with zero input.

**Scope.** Established conditionally: Definition 1 has the finite-tree model, unique up to isomorphism, and equality in that model is decidable. Open: whether C6 and the constructor conditions follow from the proposed ontology of the closed whole, and how physical states are faithfully represented. The initial-algebra result proves uniqueness among the specified free media. It does not prove that every generated algebra is free or that physical reality has no additional structure.

EF9 locates the remaining scope question in the same whole: a local result may omit distinctions that its complete record retains. Total pairing must be established separately if the claim is the unrestricted medium of Theorem 1.
