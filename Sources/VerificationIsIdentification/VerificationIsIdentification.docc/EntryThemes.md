# The Papers by Theme

This page lists the thirty-five papers under seven themes. It adds no claim. Each line gives a paper, its count of labelled statements, and the first sentence or the opening question of the paper in its own words. The grouping is editorial.

## Overview

### The core

- <doc:verification_is_identification>: 31 statements. A finite structure exists.
- <doc:solvability_criterion>: 6 statements. This paper specifies one sufficient architecture for certified search: represent a candidate family, relate it to the intended task, delimit a search region, and check supplied candidates until an adequate witness is found or the declared budget ends.
- <doc:existence_is_finite>: 16 statements. Which conditions force a form to have one recoverable finite construction, and how much of its medium do those conditions determine?
- <doc:permission_is_existence>: 6 statements. A law can be written two ways: as a list of prohibitions patrolled by a judge, or as a finite vocabulary in which the illegal cannot be said.

### Records and agreement

- <doc:record_is_system>: 13 statements. Permanence Is Resistance §5 projects the framework onto physical systems, and The Architecture of Learning Machines projects it onto learners.
- <doc:agreement_is_comparison>: 7 statements. Two independently maintained records can drift between comparisons.
- <doc:merge_is_commutation>: 4 statements. Independent edits can share their results and the evidence for those results.
- <doc:seam_is_game>: 5 statements. V=I proves a claim within its declared encoding and checking scope.
- <doc:a_verb_is_an_act>: 11 statements. A record of a system is an encoding of its facts into sentences, and the reader receives the sentences alone.

### Records, objects and acquisition

- <doc:verdict_is_invariant>: 148 statements on 14 pages. Seam Is Game judges a pair of declarations at the addresses the two sides speak about.
- <doc:acquisition_keeps_its_question>: named statements without numbers. What must an experiment leave for the next action to meet the original requirement?
- <doc:continuation_carries_the_relation>: 3 statements. After an answer has become exact, what makes the actual residue fit for the next action?

### Learning and search

- <doc:learning_is_liquid>: 27 statements on 5 pages. Learning changes what a system can distinguish and use.
- <doc:architecture_of_learning_machines>: 19 statements. An architecture earns a guarantee through a specified mechanism and its premises.
- <doc:intelligence_is_inevitable>: named statements without numbers. V=I identifies a candidate when its verified survivor set has one element.
- <doc:interference_is_intelligence>: 21 statements. The value of a question depends on what can be asked after its answer.
- <doc:play_is_proof>: 24 statements. Intelligence Is Inevitable gives a conditional coverage theorem: classes must be presented, solved and retained under a common interpretation, and cached rules need usable dispatch.
- <doc:gradient_is_alignment>: 9 statements. Local loss-descent needs an admissible lower-loss move whenever its policy has not reached a solution (GA1, GA7).
- <doc:order_is_object>: 21 statements. Fixed complete verification permits different comparison orders with the same survivor set.
- <doc:proposal_is_order>: 9 statements. A proposed candidate and a certificate about it are different objects in one system.
- <doc:basis_is_residue>: 18 statements. A library can contain both necessary generators and useful composite macros.

### Distance and geometry

- <doc:difference_is_distance>: 32 statements. A form can contain distinctions, records and reference relations within one whole.
- <doc:distance_is_cost>: 32 statements. V=I defines the loop over one test: COMPARE returns equal or not_equal.
- <doc:navigation_is_geometry>: 19 statements. Distance Is Cost gave the library a metric: every pair of entries has a distance W, computable by one traversal.
- <doc:light_is_a_ruler>: 13 statements. A light-based ruler compares emission, propagation and reception with an internal reference.
- <doc:colour_is_a_quotient>: 9 statements. Exact equality under a specified colour encoder forms a quotient.
- <doc:seeing_is_encoding>: 12 statements. A reader receives an image through a specified interaction.
- <doc:form_is_gauge>: 8 statements. A rendered artifact can have several source representations.

### Dynamics and time

- <doc:law_is_target>: 19 statements. I2 requires the identified target and the interpretation of its evidence to remain fixed.
- <doc:reading_is_order>: 5 statements. A fixed structure can be traversed in different orders.
- <doc:dynamics_is_substitution>: 11 statements. A written system is a text: named slots, one term per slot.
- <doc:program_is_path>: 11 statements. A string, a numeral, a PIN code and a program can each be represented by a finite chain of tokens.
- <doc:halt_is_ignition>: 11 statements. A record inside a fixed finite family of facts can grow strictly only finitely many times.

### Matter and medium

- <doc:permanence_is_resistance>: 129 statements on 15 pages. This paper studies a retained result as a constraint on later operations.
- <doc:interference_is_substitution>: 46 statements on 10 pages. Waves can cancel.

Two pages gather statements across papers on one theme: <doc:EntryLearning> and <doc:EntryAcquisition>. The second half of <doc:learning_is_liquid>, on the state of a medium, belongs with the last theme.
