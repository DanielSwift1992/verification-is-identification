# Admission argument for the linear Press relation

This is an argument for a declared calculus, not an inference from a finite sample. It concerns finite supported structural terms and finite source readings. It assumes the given court's local lexical/structural production judgements, DefinitionReduction replay and ApplicationCourt closed-obligation replay. It does not assume the producer, pattern search, submitted certificate or printer is correct. Physical publication and the legacy literal route are outside this relation. No nonexpressibility or external exception is claimed.

Let I be the ordered input bytes and paths, A the invocation, D the declarations admitted by the reading derivation, s the unique slot alias and t its RHS term. A rule r in the named chord declares parameters V, constant slot s, linear pattern F, template G and requirements B. An instance supplies exactly those variables of V absent from F. The transition relation R(I,A,O) holds iff every named rule is admitted in this structural fragment, exactly zero or one member structurally matches t, every matched member's substituted B has a closed application proof, and O is either the original state (zero matches) or the original byte string with only the slot's RHS replaced by G[sigma] (one match). A refused bound is non-admission, even if the triple's syntactic pattern matches. A chord with two matches does not admit an output.

## Reading

Let J(B) be the given StructuralSource judgement on the complete original UTF-8 input B, at a stated diagnostic path. It either refuses with an address or derives a lexical tape L, a complete sequence U of top-level production intervals and the declarations D read from those productions. The single scanner is scan(carrying:); it retains original byte and line coordinates. The single production reader is regions/readDeclaration. Press and roster use its strict declaration scope. Historical structural projection explicitly carries executable and conditional regions; a carried region does not establish the relations inside it. Roster separately judges its narrower admissible member/carrier relation. These are stated questions on the same reading, not separate lexers or declaration searches.

A reading witness supplies L and U, not D. Replay runs that same judgement from byte zero through the end of B and requires byte-exact lexical words and spans and exact production intervals. If J is correct for the stated grammar and these comparisons are correct, equality of the complete L and U establishes that the reconstructed D is the reading of B; no correctness of the submitted certificate or match producer is assumed. This is a conditional soundness argument. Correctness of the procedural reader/replayer itself is a named premise and remains the T0084 P4 debt. Regression cases do not prove that premise.

The leading-trivia base is included in the whole-source judgement: scanning begins in its initial state at byte zero, not at the proposed first lexeme. A first lexeme inside a leading line/block comment disagrees with L. An empty proposed tape must equal the complete tape of B. Since no certificate interval restarts the lexer, adjacent-token maximality and the final suffix are likewise inherited from J(B), rather than inferred by resetting the scanner on gaps. This replaces the former sliced lexical induction; it preserves the repaired F7c/H3 boundary without retaining a second lexical reader. The lexical newline word is normalized, but every span and line is measured on original LF, CR and CRLF bytes.

The same production reader consumes the whole tape; the witness cannot omit a production, split a statement or hide a suffix. Each declaration has an origin and token interval, from which its byte address is derived. Binding of the named rule and selected slot is reconstructed to the unique matching declaration region; duplicate declarations refuse where encountered. Generic variables are bound by their parameter declarations, and declared bound references are resolved through the shared StructuralSource/ApplicationCourt environment. This is not a claim that every name in arbitrary Swift is bound: unbounded Press constants still form a free structural signature, imports do not resolve modules, and balanced value bodies remain unchecked. Replacing that free-name signature with an explicit declaration-place signature is a separate binding debt, not something proved by the source hash. Distinct contents are established only by the declared equality/membership or constructor-disagreement relation, not inferred from byte inequality of declaration addresses.

## Matching and substitution

For a linear pattern F, structural matching against a fixed t has at most one substitution for the variables occurring in F. Proof by structural induction: a variable leaf binds the whole corresponding subtree; a constant leaf requires the same constructor; an application requires the same head and arity and applies the induction hypothesis independently to each child; a field requires the same axis and applies it to the owner. Linearity gives disjoint sets of newly bound variables across the children. The remaining variables have exactly the explicitly supplied instance values. Hence the complete sigma, when it exists, is unique. No arbitrary search strategy is an admission premise.

Replay of a positive member checks exact variable coverage, instance equality and the literal structural equation t = F[sigma]. A negative member supplies a path through equal enclosing constructors to unequal heads, arities or field axes. The checker rejects a path ending at a variable. By induction back along that path, no substitution can make F equal t. Thus negative members need no search trace and cannot conceal a matching member. Applying these judgements to the entire named chord establishes the zero/one/many boundary independently of the producer's selection.

Replay substitutes sigma into each declared requirement and asks the existing closed-obligation checker to verify its finite proof. The proof must have exactly those roots and reachable obligations, each reduction is replayed, and strict prerequisite cycles refuse. Therefore a matched outside-bound value cannot reach publication merely because a later state might be compiled. Equality requirements use the same reduction calculus; they are not nominal string heuristics.

Finally, replay constructs the sole permitted output byte string from the original prefix, G[sigma] and original suffix and compares all UTF-8 bytes. This establishes the second substitution equation and prevents changes to carried material. The zero-match case compares against the entire original byte string. Taken together, the verified reading, complete chord judgement, substituted bound proof and output equation imply R(I,A,O). Hashes bind reuse to I; direct replay still reads the actual supplied bytes, so a hash is not used as a substitute for grammar or relation proof.

## Explicit limits

The argument does not prove the Swift frontend accepts every supported fragment, next-state executable behavior, full Unicode segmentation, every error explanation, or publication atomicity. Resource exhaustion or an unsupported production is an addressed non-admission, not a counterexample to the transition relation. The v0.1/v0.2 cases exercise these mechanisms and their boundary errors; they do not prove this argument. Literal movement requires the separate step-B relation and an explicitly declared alphabet or segmentation table.
