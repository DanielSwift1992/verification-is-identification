# Continuation Carries the Relation

**Question.** After an answer has become exact, what makes the actual residue fit for the next action?
Which relation must remain, and when can an unknown relation participate without a separate reading of its stage?

**Result.** The retained boundary must answer the declared future questions and preserve their admission.
The actual source and residue must meet at each seam. One joint law can then make the receiver's entire behavior
correct while the source keeps moving. A correct current answer alone supplies neither this law nor its availability.

## A boundary is complete for a declared continuation

Fix a carrier K, questions Q on K and partial deterministic contacts O. K includes the context needed by those
questions and contacts. For each finite word w, let D_w contain exactly the states where every successive contact
is available. Include the empty word. Define

    x ≈ y  iff, for every w,
        x∈D_w ⇔ y∈D_w,
        and, when available, q(w(x))=q(w(y)) for every q∈Q.                 (E1)

**E1 (The future determines the sufficient boundary).** A reading e can answer every declared future question
and decide every word's availability exactly when ker(e)⊆≈. The quotient K/≈ carries well-defined partial
contacts and questions. This is VC15/VC47 with admission retained.

*Proof.* Each required availability test and available answer must be constant on a fiber of e.
Conversely those constants define the tests and answers on e(K). Prefixing any available contact to a word shows
that ≈ preserves its domain and relates its successors. Induction on words shows that every equivalence with
these properties is contained in ≈. Thus the quotient has representative-independent contacts. ∎

The condition gives decoders for the future tests. An arbitrary sufficient e can retain extra distinctions whose
updates are not determined by e itself. It therefore need not carry a common induced contact.
The quotient does. An executor and a physical reading of either description require their own construction.

E1 is relative to Q and O. A new contact into an omitted role can expose a distinction that was previously
irrelevant. Enlarging the family requires the corresponding refinement. There is no claim that K/≈ is finite,
computable, or a description of every possible continuation of reality.

For one fixed joining relation R⊆A×B, LQ13 gives the least admission boundary by equal partner profiles:
N(a)={b:(a,b)∈R}. Equal profiles preserve admission and refusal at that cut. Distinct joinings can have the same
endpoints. Questions about those joinings require retaining their distinctions as well.

## The residue retains the original source binding

Let P be source prefixes, F future continuations, and J⊆P×F the actual admitted relation. Every p has at least
one partner. Let q(p) be the original question. A boundary c is complete for this relation when some S satisfies

    (p,f)∈J ⇔ (c(p),f)∈S.                                              (E2a)

Suppose the late answer r is exact on every admitted pair:

    (p,f)∈J ⇒ r(f)=q(p).                                                (E2b)

**E2 (An exact late answer cannot emerge from a boundary that merged its source distinction).**
Then q=d∘c on the reached image, as in Matter M72 and RN27a–b.

*Proof.* If c(p)=c(p′), choose a partner f of p. E2a makes it a partner of p′ too.
E2b gives q(p)=r(f)=q(p′). These common values define d. ∎

The boundary need not be one separate memory register. A local reading l=e∘c can coincide while q differs.
The joint relation then retains a distinction that this local reading omits. The argument supplies no currently
available decoder d. The physical result can itself carry the distinction without an added outside reader.

Availability of a continuation does not promise its occurrence. Pairwise separators do not supply one successful
process for an unknown input. RN28 distinguishes a common available process, inevitable contact and guaranteed
recovery of q. A promise of recovery needs reaching the correct reading in every admitted complete maximal
continuation of the selected process, with a nonempty family at every relevant cut.

Retaining q also does not require a new historical mark at every act. RN29's swap (a,b)↦(b,a) can expose b and
return the full pair after two steps. A claim of nonreturn needs its independently proved nonperiodic factor or
a new and old contrast that every allowed continuation retains. Neither follows from future availability alone.

Composition must use the actual seam. VC14 joins two certified squares through the same intermediate source
and observation. Equal intermediate images of different sources do not by themselves form one execution.
VI U9 derives the changed source relation after an action instead of continuing to use the old one.
U10a additionally retains the requested source binding: making an equal pair and keeping the old source elsewhere
does not make that pair an execution of the original request.

The least additional residue is relative to what is already retained (VI U6). With retained a and requested b,
both b and a XOR b suffice on all bit pairs. A least standalone residue need not exist, although the joint
information needed for (a,b) is fixed. All such joint views use the same source occurrence.

## An unknown relation can work throughout the receiver's process

The following is the existing construction RU2–3a. D and E have the same finite dimension d≥2.
M is the rest of the receiver. S is the source, possibly infinite dimensional, and R is an arbitrary reference.
The full initial density operator ρ on SDEMR can have arbitrary correlations.

Let X=Swap_DE, κ>0 and K_EM be a bounded self-adjoint law of the receiver. Set

    B=κ(I−X)+K_EM.

Let 𝒰 be a supplied unitary relation on SD, W=𝒰†, and H_S a self-adjoint source law bounded below.
The joint law is supplied as

    H=W(H_S+B)W†,       Dom H=W Dom(H_S+B).                              (E3a)

Tensor identities are suppressed. W need not commute with H_S. The required receiver behavior is independently
specified: the behavior under B with the transformed joint input 𝒰ρ𝒰†.

**E3 (The receiver uses the relation without a stage signal).** For every ρ and t,

    Tr_SD[e^(−itH)ρe^(itH)]
       =Tr_SD[e^(−itB)𝒰ρ𝒰†e^(itB)].                                   (E3b)

The equality is true branch by branch under every finite sequence of receiver instruments on EM,
including adaptive choices based on their results. Local instruments on the retained R are permitted too.

*Proof.* The exact propagator is W e^(−itH_S)e^(−itB) W†.
Every receiver or reference instrument commutes with W and H_S. In each branch, adjacent W†W cancel.
The source propagators commute with B and these instruments, so their product moves to the outer edge.
The partial trace removes the outer conjugations by W and the source propagator.
The remaining unnormalized branch state is precisely the B branch on 𝒰ρ𝒰†. For instruments with several Kraus
operators the equality is true term by term and then by summation. Equal branch traces give equal probabilities.
The same branch results therefore permit the same adaptive choices. ∎

This is a finite proof for all such finite receiver histories. It does not compute their steps in advance.
No known blank input, independent initial parts, reset or separate reading of the source was assumed.
Tracing SD compares the receiver's behavior. It does not physically erase those parts or their correlations.

The source can change its own relation during the process. Equality of the receiver behavior does not force
the source's numerical readings to remain constant. Because the theorem accepts every joint input, the actual
residue can be its next input. That next application concerns the relation at the new boundary.
Repeating the original 𝒰 promise requires a further preservation law.

RU5 proves necessity of the conjugated form only in its declared smooth second-order elliptic class on a compact
source manifold without boundary, with all receiver questions and all inputs required. It does not classify every
physical implementation or every partial question. For κ=0 its exchange-based necessity argument is unavailable.

## Matching a result is weaker than matching the whole residue

RU10 compares E3a with a supplied controlled episode: first apply 𝒰 on SD, then evolve under H_S+B.
Its full state σ satisfies

    ρ_RU(t)=W σ(t)W†.                                                    (E4)

The receiver histories agree because W acts only on SD. The whole states generally differ.
An available final W gives the same full residue on the selected boundary.
Receiver-only equality does not justify the next contact if it reaches SD.
If the question concerns the finite-duration control itself, that control must also enter the joint model.
Naming the exact prefix and the final W supplies neither their controller nor their physical implementation.

A moving frame requires an equally explicit source binding. HO4/HOR4 makes the relative port geometry part of
the J question. Factoring out all rotations identifies u with Ju by a changed question. It does not answer the
original one. Nor does covariance remove an unknown shift: RW(s)=W_R(s)R gives
R(W(s)Ju₀)=W_R(s)R(Ju₀), not R(Ju₀). HOR21's corrected family R_δ=W_R(−δ)R works when the actual shift δ,
the initial frame and that family remain available in the receiver's joint relation.

HO8c/HOR20 concerns instantaneous freezing under an unchanged free translation law. On the full L² ball, even
a small timing error gives no uniform small field error. A smooth shutdown can instead fix the integrated
exposure under another supplied law. The clock's preparation, work and residue then belong to that new contract.
The local lemma is not a theorem that all exact continuation requires one exact stopping instant.

OA81 keeps the obligations together: the question distinguished, the certification rule, preparation,
actual interaction, reciprocal effects, applicability to the reached residue, and price in one declared contract.
The next action is justified by that reached relation. A reused proof name cannot supply it.

## A field handoff uses an unknown receiver load

HPU77 gives a concrete instance of continuation on an actual changed residue.
The contract is one output Q_out=Ju₀ with the old literal memory m₀, under the supplied HP.4 law.
It does not require the ring's final field to be constant.

Take the ring field at the first successful cut as Ju₀=a+f, where a is its mean and f has zero mean.
The receiver segment Σ=[0,ℓ) initially contains an unknown zero-mean h with ||h||₂≤W.
The line is empty outside Σ. The clock preparation, disjoint control bands and paused ring are supplied.
Let ||u₀||₂≤U, p_in∈[0,p_max], and let the full Z₀ satisfy the former common bounds of the HPU77 input.
The same-source whole contains both f and h. Neither is read or reset.

On the exchange band, the direct product brackets −∂ₓ and the supplied nonlocal antiderivative coupling give

    df/dτ=−w_Σ,      dw_Σ/dτ=f,
    f_τ=cosτ f−sinτ h,      w_Σ,τ=sinτ f+cosτ h.                         (E5a)

Here τ is the integrated pulse κ of the actual clock. The strict reserve conditions HPU.12 keep that clock
on its free-band law. At τ=π/2, the line receives f and the ring receives a−h.
The same Casimir coupling then adds a to Σ and the compensating −a to another segment.
The subsequent line translation has the declared port binding

    Q_out(x)=w(x+d_w)=Ju₀(x),       0≤x<ℓ,     d_w=∫λ_w dθ.             (E5b)

The old load has moved to the ring with a minus sign. It was not erased, and it was not mixed into Q_out.
The quarter-turn is an operator identity for all admitted f and h. The argument does not infer their values
or compute a separate trajectory for each mode. The nonlocal coupling and the common clock program are conditions.

The price depends on the actual exchange. With A the admitted antiderivative,

    B₀=⟨Af,h⟩,     P=𝓔+κB₀,     |B₀|≤(ℓ/√2)UW.                       (E5c)

The final κ is zero, so the net work on this pair is zero. The intermediate reserve excursion is generally
nonzero. After the output leaves, the next ring act receives v=a−h, and its working reserve is

    P_work=P_init−ℓa²−||h||₂²/2 ≥ P_init−U²−W²/2.                       (E5d)

Its required norm bound is ||v||₂≤√(U²+W²). Reusing the old P_init would omit the activation of the actual h.
HPU.12 protects the first act, the entire exchange, the mean copy, the output activation and the later tail.
These are sufficient reserve bounds, not a minimum price.

The subsequent ring ends are Jⁿ(a−h), with old m₀, for n≥1.
The line continues carrying the transferred Ju₀ in its moving frame. No new exchange is made on every act.
The complete outgoing packet and its compensating packet have energy

    𝓔=P_init+p_in+E₀(u₀),     E_w=E₀(u₀)+ℓa²/2,
    𝓔′=𝓔−E_w=P_init+p_in−ℓa²/2,     E₀(u)=||u||₂²/2.                    (E5f)

The full Source follows the changed energy 𝓔′, not the old 𝓔:

    Z_(1+n)=exp{−i[(1+2n)ΩT_I+(𝓔+2n𝓔′)d_I(z)]}Z₀.                   (E5e)

The n=0 cut is the first successful end before the handoff and has its own field Ju₀ and energy 𝓔.
The phase in E5e is an actual residue. Its dependence on n alone does not establish an injective record of n.

This contract admits unknown bounded h with a known zero mean. A nonzero mean, an unbounded load with one fixed
reserve, or unknown line contents outside Σ lie outside it. Requiring the ring to end at the old constant a
would force h=0. The coupling, program, preparation and port remain supplied outside the original D_H.
The provided Hamiltonian has no established global nonnegativity claim. E5 does not acquire those resources
or settle LG28d.

## Scope

These laws relate the original question, the actual residue and the declared next contacts. They do not settle the open LG28d obligation on the former O_E with the given χ, p₀ and complete Z_*: every late success set S_n must have empty interior on that one domain. Nor do they acquire an executor through the original D_H. The field question remains u/J together with the literal memory. The receiver behavior in E3 is a different declared contract. Its quantum construction supplies no transfer to UR10e.
