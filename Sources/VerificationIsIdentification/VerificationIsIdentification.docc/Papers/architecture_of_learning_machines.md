# The Architecture of Learning Machines (Projection)

An architecture earns a guarantee through a specified mechanism and its premises. A verifier, a store and a generator have different obligations. Naming all three does not establish soundness, complete coverage, reproducibility or efficient learning. Conversely, absence of an append-only store does not force forgetting, and absence of a formal verifier does not force every output to be wrong.

This paper projects VI onto learning architectures by asking what is checked, what is retained, how proposals arrive and what a certificate can be used for. The central result is a conditional wrapper theorem. It separates correctness of accepted outputs from the chance and cost of obtaining one. It does not claim that every learner is already an instance of VI or that adding a gate solves an arbitrary learning problem.

## §0. Declared Semantics

Fix a task τ, a set of possible outputs Y and the subset C_τ of outputs correct under its declared semantics. A total checker accepts a set A_τ ⊆ Y. It is sound when A_τ ⊆ C_τ and complete for this output interface when C_τ ⊆ A_τ. Decidability means termination of checking, not either inclusion.

An output may include evidence, such as a proof of a formal statement. Then Y contains answer/evidence pairs and completeness concerns that interface. A checker that accepts suitable proof objects need not accept every correct bare answer. A check against examples establishes agreement on those examples. Connecting that agreement to an unknown law requires the model and representability premises of System CS3.

The generator, checker, store, source, seed and controller are internal parts of the chosen whole. Separate software roles do not imply an external source of reality. The model below still declares what each role can read. Knowing the whole model for analysis does not give its unknown actual state to the executing generator.

| Component | Required evidence | What follows under that evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Checker | Sound accepted set for a declared claim | Accepted outputs satisfy that claim |
| Complete checking interface | Every relevant correct proposal is accepted | Checking does not discard those proposals |
| Retaining store | Sound keyed update and fixed interpretation | Recorded claims remain available in that scope |
| Generator and scheduler | Proposal law or actual sequence, fairness and budget | A separately derived acquisition probability or bound |
| Representation | Domain, question and interpretation of evidence | Meaning and applicability of the certificate |
| Search policy | Available queries, costs and history inputs | A measurable efficiency objective |

## §1. Guarantees Have Separate Premises

**AI1 (A sufficient construction is not an iff classification).** Sound checking plus emission only after acceptance suffices for scoped output correctness. Sound retaining updates suffice for preserving accessible keyed records under fixed semantics. Neither implication proves that this construction is necessary for every system with the corresponding behavior.

A deterministic correct computation can recompute answers without retaining a comparison cache (System GC4). A mutable register can preserve its value. A deterministic program can reproduce its output without a checker. A program can abstain by returning no answer. Such examples refute the earlier universal iff claims about memory, reproducibility and abstention. Abstention alone also gives no coverage.

Certification does not by itself prove that a basis is minimal or transferable. Minimality requires a declared generating operation and cost, and transfer requires compatibility of the new context (Basis B6–B8, Verdict VC35–VC36). If a checker is unsound or the encoded model misses the actual law, the presence of a gate does not repair that missing premise.

## §2. Projection onto Systems

**AI2 (A generator and a complete system have different boundaries).** A neural model can be used as a proposal component, coupled to a proof checker, a persistent store, a retrieval system or a tool environment. Which guarantees the resulting system has depends on that composition. Mutable parameters permit changes in behavior but do not prove that every update forgets a task. Deterministic inference and reproducible training are also different claims, each requiring the relevant inputs and execution conditions to be fixed.

A claim about a particular model's failure rate, representational basis or reproducibility needs evidence about that model and setup. The label LLM does not prove the absence of every checking mechanism or the impossibility of a scoped certificate. The former universal classification as pure liquid is withdrawn.

**AI3 (Reinforcement and formal checking can coexist).** A reward may score an estimate, an observed outcome or a formally checked proof. Its use in reinforcement learning does not decide which of these it is. A policy's estimated value is different from a certificate of a particular completed proof. [AlphaProof](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12999475/) combines reinforcement learning with formal proof search in Lean, providing an example of these roles operating together. It does not establish every VI premise for every task.

| Architecture role | What to inspect |
|---|---|
| Exact solver or tablebase | Correctness of construction, queried domain and representation |
| Neural or other statistical proposal | Which outputs carry evidence and which are unchecked predictions |
| Reinforcement learning | What the reward evaluates and what is actually certified |
| Retrieval | Integrity, provenance and applicability of retrieved records |
| Proof generator with checker | Formal statement, accepted proof language and trusted checking scope |
| VI witness | Concrete type constraints, instantiated type witnesses, executable transition and negative cases |

These are inspection questions, not a scorecard that derives a system's behavior from its name.

## §3. Search Quality and Correctness

**AI4 (Search performance needs its own comparison).** Positive aggregate alignment does not ensure successful descent or generalization (Gradient GA6). A locally informative query can worsen optimal continuation cost (InI S3a). Therefore no speed or accuracy advantage follows from adding an alignment coordinate to the projection.

Within AI6's sound-checking model, changing the generator cannot create an accepted wrong output if the checker and output path remain sound. It can change how often a correct proposal appears, how long it takes to check, and whether a budget is exhausted. These effects need a common workload, stopping rule and cost model. Correctness and efficiency have separate proof obligations, but a change in one component can affect both costs and coverage.

## §4. The Phase Vocabulary

**AI5 (A resolution map requires a fixed criterion).** A library can resolve some questions and leave others open. Liquid LQ2 describes this map under fixed question semantics and sound retaining updates. A successful repeat can add no new record, and a new record can settle no new question (LQ3). Neither an advancing physical front nor laminar search follows from these facts.

Liquid, crystal and front in that acquisition account name declared resolution and retention properties. The implementation must specify what is stored, what is derivable, what is retrievable and at what cost. Infinite reach may have a finite generative description. No general claim that neural systems never crystallize follows from the vocabulary.

Liquid LQ12 studies a different state: the learner's medium of admissible forms and permitted rearrangements. Two media can admit the same forms and the same common truths while allowing different changes between them. Learning may therefore change a relation of access or rearrangement without adding a new admissible answer. Conversely, a tightly coupled admission relation need not prevent movement through its forms. A useful architecture must distinguish preserved claims from the transformations under which they are preserved. The operation and its context are part of this question, rather than a fixed external checker. The component argument of LQ12 supplies an invariant for reversible rearrangements. It does not assign a physical phase to a model from its accuracy, parameter count or memory entropy.

LQ13 gives a concrete criterion for changing a component while retaining its partners. Its compatibility profile must be included in the replacement's profile, and a uniform reversible replacement preserves that profile exactly. The least complete interface keeps both admitted and forbidden combinations. Separate compressed representations of the two sides need not share one identical code: equality alone forces a rectangle condition that the vacancy contact {00,01,10} violates. A learner that merges these forms solely to make accepted pairs match would also accept the forbidden 11. This supplies an adversarial test of a representation, beyond testing its answers on the accepted examples.

The architectural target is therefore to preserve the needed contact relation under compression and revision. A source-bound proof can cover an entire constructor family without testing every instance. Sampled successful contacts alone do not establish that coverage. LQ14 supplies a second check: a claimed need for a large simultaneous update must survive possible detours through internal context changes. Its coordination bound concerns admitted constructions, without predicting training time or a neural optimization advantage.

IiS20–IiS21 add an adversarial criterion for combining learned claims: do their certificates admit one shared realization? There are finite transformation families whose every proper subfamily is realizable but whose whole is impossible, even with unrestricted output memory. Pairwise probabilities and all matched mixture entropies can agree across the two families. Independent local witnesses therefore cannot certify the common law. The exact cycle regime has a finite common certificate from a spanning forest and the remaining edges. `ResidentTransportContact` and `JointEvidence` retain the actual source assignment across finite contact families; joining witnesses with different assignments is refused. This suggests a concrete acquisition target: settle the missing shared relations, rather than count how many isolated claims already pass. The required graph coverage and the meaning of its edges still need evidence. No gain in neural training efficiency follows from this certificate alone.

The proposed components can themselves be tested. On fixed finite addresses, LQ15 derives the unique finest independently recombinable blocks from the admitted relation. It prevents choosing a finer modular decomposition that admits a forbidden splice. For a declared recombination relation, LQ16 instead recovers its two roles from exact self-contact and preservation of each role under further contact. Its laws force single-valued results without assuming that every proposed relation is functional. Training or synthesis could propose such a decomposition; acceptance would have to preserve domains and results of the actual construction family. Agreement on successful examples alone misses the explicit refusal counterexample in LQ16.

The resulting target is an exact contract for a representation change: recover the roles the operation actually uses, preserve their allowed combinations, and account for the complementary result when recovery of the source is required. Three source-bound equality contacts suffice for the corresponding reversible completion; an arbitrary contact needs a separate check. This gives concrete invalid representations and updates to reject. It does not yet supply a learning procedure that discovers the contact family from incomplete empirical evidence.

M70 makes the resource side necessary for a claim of continued use. Every finite local deterministic table can occur for a prepared finite horizon inside some finite reversible whole. A fitting local rule and unchanged joint entropy therefore do not establish that the same local rule remains available indefinitely in the actual carrier. The learner's retained source, prepared record space and internal control must be included when certifying reuse. An unbounded record constructor supplies another admitted form at each extension in its grammar; it does not prove that a fixed physical store has that capacity. Law LT3–LT6 and LT15 now carry this distinction into the scope of identifying a dynamics.

## §5. The Conditional Wrapper Theorem

**AI6 (Soundness, coverage and retention are separate).** Consider a fixed task, an actual proposal sequence y₁,y₂,… and a checker that accepts A_τ. A wrapper checks each processed proposal and emits only accepted proposals, or returns no certified answer.

(i) **Sound output.** If A_τ ⊆ C_τ, every emitted proposal is correct in the declared scope. This follows by set inclusion. It does not require independent trials, a good generator or an append-only cache. If the emitted payload differs from the checked one, or a certificate is applied outside its scope, the premise no longer establishes that output's correctness.

(ii) **Coverage for the same proposal prefix.** Let E_i mean that proposal i is correct, and F_i that it is correct and accepted. Always F_i ⊆ E_i. If checking is complete on the proposals under consideration, F_i = E_i. For the same first n proposals processed to completion, the event of seeing at least one correct accepted proposal therefore equals the event of seeing at least one correct proposal. No independence is needed for this event identity.

Soundness alone does not imply completeness. A checker accepting nothing is sound and certifies nothing. A proof checker may reject a true claim whose required proof was not supplied. The wrapper theorem cannot turn the probability of producing a bare true answer into the probability of producing an accepted proof without a bridge between these events.

(iii) **Acquisition law.** Write h_i = Pr(F_i | no earlier F_j), when the conditioning event has positive probability. Repeated conditional probability gives:

```
Pr(no accepted correct proposal through n) = ∏_{i=1}^n (1 − h_i).
```

Once survival probability is zero, later hazards can be assigned arbitrarily without changing the product. Independent trials with constant success probability p give 1 − (1 − p)^n for success by n. More generally, a conditional bound h_i ≥ ε > 0 while failure persists gives a success lower bound 1 − (1 − ε)^n. Equal marginal success probabilities do not suffice: choose a fair bit once and repeat its proposal forever. Every trial then has success probability 1/2, while eventual success still has probability 1/2.

(iv) **Retention.** If the store adds accepted scoped claims through a sound retaining update with immutable keys, its historical record grows by set inclusion. This is an implementation premise. A repeated certificate need not add anything, and a stored claim need not remain applicable after changing its task interpretation. An observation can remain true as a record while a prediction based on a revised model loses its warrant.

(v) **Cost boundary.** Equal proposal count does not mean equal elapsed budget. With proposals (wrong, correct), generation cost one, checking cost one and budget two, the raw sequence can contain the correct second proposal. The wrapper can process only the first. A sound complete checker thus preserves coverage over equal processed prefixes but can reduce coverage within equal time budgets. Feedback can also change later proposals, in which case the two sequences need an explicit coupling before their coverage is compared.

A finite closed whole can include a prepared proposal tape and record register. Reversible writing can preserve accepted events during a bounded window, then restore the prepared register. Closure alone does not supply an unbounded IID stream or permanent readiness.

**Worked lifecycle (CS3 and VC35–VC36).** With two Boolean inputs, consider the full family {00,01,10,11}. Observing input 0 return 0 leaves {00,01}, so an answer certificate for input 0 can be reused with no further source observation. Adding the sound observation that input 1 returns 1 leaves {01}, a subset of the old compatible set, and preserves the first answer.

In the smaller declared family of constant tables {00,11}, the same initial observation conditionally certifies that input 1 returns 0. Widening the family to all four tables invalidates reuse of that certificate: both second answers are now possible. A new observation can resolve them. A source 01 is consistent with the initial observation but violates the smaller family's premise. A checker of the conditional certificate cannot detect that discrepancy from the initial observation alone.

A certificate that establishes q = a throughout a nonempty compatible set K also establishes q = a on any nonempty K′ ⊆ K. The implication is restriction of the same predicate to a smaller domain. Equal answers under a different question or representation require their own connecting relation. `RefineAnswer` supplies a constructive evidence bridge. `JoinedFactorization` and its `AddressedReading` endpoints compose compatible representation changes (VC36).

**Acquisition and retained access (LQ3, S3a, AI6 and VC40–VC42).** `AcquireAnswer` factors a source reading through its recorded fact. `RefineAnswer` carries a question through evidence that recovers its previous observation. `AnswerInTrace` names the existing decoder of a compatible certificate. Its type is F.Right, with the actual trace memory as its input. The constant-table family derives the second answer from the first fact. An unrestricted pair needs a distinguishing second reading, while its first answer remains available through the retained prefix.

## §6. What a Saturation Map Measures

**AI7 (Current coverage differs from a retaining ledger).** Fix a finite nonempty task set D, checker scope, run conditions and evaluation budget. Current checked coverage C_t is the set of tasks whose evaluated outputs at version t pass. It need not grow across model updates or repeated stochastic runs.

A historical ledger H_t = ⋃_{s≤t} C_s grows by definition when the certificates and their applicability are retained under fixed task semantics. It measures demonstrated past coverage, not necessarily the current model's unaided ability. A separate reuse mechanism is needed to turn old evidence into a present answer. Changing D, checker meaning or evaluation budget changes the measurement.

A total checker does not make a possibly nonterminating generator total. Per-task coverage is decidable only when the evaluation procedure has a terminating execution rule, such as a fixed budget and an explicit timeout result. For stochastic systems, one run's map is a sample, not its exact success probability. Comparisons require matched tasks, budgets and checking scope.

Compilation checks the relevant language constraints, a test suite checks its executed assertions, a schema validator checks the schema, and a proof checker checks a formal derivation. Those are different claims. Calling every pass a certificate of the unknown real-world answer would erase the distinction this map needs.

## §7. The Type-Level Witness Boundary

**AI8 (An acquired image can answer a family of future questions).** Suppose
the permitted transitions have certified image updates, each target question
factors through its final image, and the initial image has been acquired in
a readable record. `QuestionAlongTransition` derives the future question's
factorization through that initial image (VC45). `AnswerOnRefinement` then
transports the answer to the acquired fact (VC41). Every constructed path
with the required endpoints admits this derivation.

In the two-swap fragment, VC44 establishes closure from two generic
preservation relations. The same recorded complete image answers alpha
after any permitted finite mixed continuation. A separate learned entry for
every word is unnecessary for this guarantee. The operation word remains
part of the question, while the decoder reads the original acquired fact.
This is saturation of a specified family of relations, even when the family
contains arbitrarily long words.

The guarantee concerns sufficiency and derivability. It does not assign a
runtime or energy cost to reading a long type expression. It also does not
predict which operation an unspecified controller will choose. Extending
the allowed interactions can require a more informative image. The initial
alpha fact alone cannot answer the mixed continuation exposing the third
coordinate. M42 relates the sufficient image to conditional uncertainty,
while keeping whole entropy and physical acquisition separate.

For partial interactions, the image must also preserve where each action
is available (VC46–VC47). Agreement on every successful result does not
establish that domain condition. A complete acquired image can supply the
resident equality required by `MatchedSwapTransition`, and its future answer
still uses VC41 and VC45. After the step, availability concerns the successor's
fields. A record of earlier success does not authorize repetition. A total
extension's computed answer also does not establish that the partial action
is available. Its differently specified question must remain explicit.

**AI9 (Saturation is relative to the interaction family).** In the binary guarded-swap fragment, six classes suffice for the present alpha question and every available continuation (VC49). VC50–VC52 derive those classes and answers from a previously acquired complete image. The derived decoder's input is the fact, while the acquisition relation remains tied to its actual source. There is no separate observation of the source in this derivation.

Adding the total swap requires eight classes (VC54). Two sources that shared the old blocked class now give different future answers. If the complete fact was retained, the new question can be answered from that fact by another certified factorization. If only the old class is accessible, no decoder on that class can distinguish the pair. Additional evidence, or a justified restriction of the source family, is then required.

This separates two possible changes in a learner: finding a new consequence of retained evidence and acquiring a distinction that the accessible evidence does not determine. The finite example proves the distinction between these cases. It does not specify how a learner discovers the new factorization or which physical experiment can supply missing evidence. Those are further relations to establish. A minimal representation for a fixed family also need not be a good retention policy when future interactions are unknown.

**AI10 (an internal acquisition can target the unresolved classes).** VC55–VC57 supply the next relation left open by AI9. The participant's old ready class already determines the full binary triple. At a blocked class, a prepared reading of beta supplies the missing distinction. The choice to read depends only on the accessible old class. The channel address contains the question and context, not the hidden answer. Its output fact is bound to the same source as the old trace.

The old class remains readable in the new memory prefix. The completed image then supports every certified mixed future by VC52. Under the explicit uniform preparation of M43, the extra channel is used on half the sources. The accessible source information increases by one half bit while whole Shannon entropy remains three bits. This is an exact finite-model result, not a measured training advantage or a physical cost bound.

The inverse whole transition makes the newly acquired beta inaccessible again without losing the source. Repeating the forward acquisition while keeping all old records would require further prepared capacity, as M44 explains. Thus finding the missing relation, acquiring its answer and maintaining access to that answer require separate constructions. No independent observation is required for a consequence already determined by the ready class.

**AI11 (workspace reuse and knowledge reuse have different conditions).** VC58–VC59 connect an acquisition decision from existing memory to preparation from a resident reserve and retention at an accessible archive address. The instrument can return to its prepared state while its answer remains available. The decision, acquisition source, resource input and retained evidence are checked relations. The final decoder accepts the old class and archived beta fact, without receiving the source as its input.

This suggests a concrete separation in a learning architecture: deriving consequences of available evidence, acquiring a missing distinction, and relocating evidence so that an instrument can be reused. Only the second changes which members of an unresolved class can be distinguished in this example. The third changes the answer's address. Once the complete-image factorization exists, the declared future questions need no additional beta acquisition even if the reserve is empty. Conversely, resetting the entire accessible participant to an old unresolved state would remove the distinction needed by the answer.

M45 counts reserve slots separately from information gained. This is not an argument that each training update must append a permanent new memory cell, nor a demonstrated speed or quality advantage over an existing learner. The static result is the connected acquisition and access contract. Discovering an unknown adequate channel, implementing its physical coupling and comparing learning performance remain separate tasks.

**AI12 (reusing evidence requires its applicability relation).** VC62 gives a precise form of reuse: once two addressed applications on the same resident source are certified to agree, the existing observation and decoder for one answer the other without another acquisition. Prepared recording and archive access make that shared fact available inside the represented participant. The source relation remains part of the justification even though the memory payload need not duplicate both sources.

VC63 prevents a circular use of this rule. In the binary pair example, a first-side fact is enough for the common answer on the compatible domain, but the same fact can come from a source outside that domain. Therefore a learner cannot treat possession of the fact as evidence that the transfer rule applies. Identical evidence plus identical additional storage remains indistinguishable across those cases. The missing work is to obtain a relation or observation that separates them, or justify a narrower source family. Within an already established family, reusing the fact requires neither a second measurement nor a second fact slot. These are static applicability and acquisition results, not a measured advantage over another learning architecture.

**AI13 (applicability can be acquired and then reused).** VC64 completes the missing step in AI12 for two resident component readings. Their acquired facts share one actual trace. Comparing the memory decoders derives agreement or a positive source-answer distinction, without assuming the source answers agree. The classifier takes memory alone. Its three images retain precisely the common answer or its obstruction, while the full record preserves the ordered facts and sources.

The acquired agreement can then license VC62's reuse. It is evidence about these addressed applications on this source, not an unrestricted law about unseen sources or later changed states. VC65 also gives a precise acquisition bound: when all four binary pairs are still possible and only component readings are allowed, one first-side fact always leaves a relevant distinction unresolved. One other-side reading is necessary and sufficient. A retained same-source second-side fact already supplies that distinction, so comparison then requires no new acquisition. This connects discovery of applicability with subsequent reuse in the static relation language. It does not establish how often useful relations occur in another learning problem or a training-performance advantage.

**AI14 (an intervention changes what retained knowledge must determine).** VC66 transports the acquired common-answer class through side exchange. Its three classes suffice for every repetition of that operation. VC67 adds an exchange between the first answer coordinate and a prepared resident zero. The old failure class now needs its two orientations separated. The two original acquired facts already supply that distinction, so a future answer follows from them through the commuting square without another measurement.

The fact remains about its original source. The future question names the operation, endpoint and required preparation. After the zero moves out of the reserve, repeating the same exchange can restore the displaced answer. A learner that keeps only the updated visible pair then omits a distinction needed by that continuation. This gives a precise retention question: which hidden or historical distinctions determine the allowed next results? The answer depends on the interaction family and on available evidence, not only on the present prediction. The construction establishes these static conditions, without a performance comparison between learners.

**AI15 (an exact learned representation can be smaller than the state it must acquire).** VC69's comparison table has fifteen values over sixteen binary working configurations and determines every common-answer continuation under the two internal exchanges. The two merged configurations remain indistinguishable under that complete continuation family. Keeping their orientation adds no predictive distinction for this interface. VC70 shows exactly what reopens it: a direct component question.

VC71 still requires four component readings to acquire the fifteen-class object from unrestricted unknown coordinates. Every unread coordinate permits a flip that changes the required class. This separates minimal retained representation from the information and channels needed to obtain it. In the established prepared family, the old two alpha facts already determine the four working coordinates. The compiler derives the representation and a mixed continuation's answer from that old memory through existing factorization and transition constructors. No new source fact or runtime prediction loop enters that derivation.

The architectural obligation is concrete: state the questions, certify the representation's updates on the admitted operations, and retain source-bound evidence sufficient to recover that representation. When a new question separates an old class, identify its missing observation or an already accessible record. A finite proof basis can cover an unbounded family of constructed continuations while the memory payload omits distinctions that none of them uses. This establishes exact reuse within the declared grammar. Compilation cost, hardware cost and learning quality require their own measurements.

VC72 supplies a further boundary without changing the question: one new internal connection makes the formerly omitted orientation consequential. If the original trace remains accessible, its old fact supplies the refinement. If only the fifteen-value image was retained, its all-B class needs one new coordinate reading. Thus a change in possible interactions can reopen learning even while the vocabulary of requested answers remains fixed.

**AI16 (a finite calibration basis can support arbitrary new data in a certified law family).** VC73–VC76 turn a supplied interaction law into a recoverable object of observation. In the four-address routing family, two complete binary probes identify the selected input at each output. The same acquired signatures then determine routing on arbitrary structural data values, provided the new input is read and the same routing applies. Saturation here covers the declared coordinate-selection rule for all its data arguments. It does not require storing one example for every possible data tuple.

Three relations do the work: calibration connects a probe record to a routing signature, a source relation ties the new application to that routing, and a data reading supplies its actual new argument. `RoutingApplicationFromProbes` and `EvidenceBesideInput` compose these relations without a final-output equality premise or a hidden unread data parameter. The output can then feed the existing common-answer table. None of these examples adds a runtime prediction procedure or a new protocol to the core.

The distinction has practical content even before a performance claim. A new data value inside the certified routing grammar calls for input acquisition and reuse of the law evidence. A change in the routing or an expansion to gates depending on several inputs calls for new law evidence or a stronger family witness. A changing internal schedule can produce a record that looks exactly like a fixed permutation's record, so syntactic validity of the learned representation is insufficient to certify its continued applicability. The representation's source relation matters at reuse.

M50 makes the temporal requirement explicit. A fact can remain a distinction of the complete history while ceasing to be recoverable through the learner's later interface. Reusable knowledge therefore requires the same earlier question to factor through that later reading. Historical occurrence, validity of the proposition and present access to its witness supply different parts of the learning claim. A certificate about the full history cannot silently substitute for the learner's actual input. M52 strengthens this boundary: mutually agreeing records can remain in agreement while jointly losing their information about the earlier source. Even a decrease in the entropy of those records can accompany an increase in uncertainty about that source. A learning objective based on agreement or reduced record variability therefore needs the question-preserving relation across its update. M53 shows that reciprocal response between components also permits joint mergers. M54 supplies the positive construction: faithful later witnesses for each required earlier question can be joined on their common image. A learner needs coverage of its declared questions, while recovery of the complete represented source requires coverage of all its declared coordinates. This locates the preservation obligation without requiring every interface to copy the entire world.

VC76 supplies two prepared resident instruments, their actual intermediate states and accessible records. M48 counts the decrease in conditional law uncertainty while the retained whole preserves its probability weights. This separates finishing the acquisition of a scoped rule from reading each new argument, retaining the evidence, and establishing stability of the mechanism. It supplies a checked finite demonstration and generic data-domain proof, without an empirical training-speed comparison or a claim that ordinary language-model behavior is a routing law.

VC77 makes stability a transport relation rather than a demand that the entire mechanism state freeze. A certified internal change can preserve the routing image and all its uses on read new inputs. Another internal exchange replaces the routing from a reserve, which old law-only evidence does not determine. M49 gives a finite case where uncertainty about the current law increases while its old record and all whole distinctions survive. A learner must therefore specify not only the law family it can identify, but the changes under which its acquired law representation remains sufficient.

**AI17 (an acquired law can reduce the required input interface).** VC78 derives which new input coordinates a fixed routing needs. When all four outputs select the first input, the decoder accepts the law record and only that first value. Three unused values disappear from its argument type, and even the shape of an unused source branch ceases to be required. The source can retain those distinctions while this interface omits them.

The law therefore does more than produce an answer after complete data acquisition: it can justify a smaller input question. Under channels that read one independently unknown coordinate at a time, a permutation needs all four coordinates and a repeated-first route needs only the first. If the law changes, its input requirement must be derived again or transported through a certificate preserving it. The checked identity counterexample has the same restricted observation on two inputs but different outputs.

This gives a concrete role for a saturated law representation: determine the data interface needed by the requested consequence, acquire any missing arguments, then reuse the certified decoder. The finite model establishes that reduction for its declared routing and channel families. It does not presume that a new input's unread coordinates are zero, absent from reality or known from the law record.

VC79 locates the sensor's role in that interface. The same acquired response law can belong to different module/frame decompositions. It remains useful for predicting the specified composite's outputs, while identifying a module separately requires an internal calibration relation. The scope of a learned law therefore includes the path by which its answers reach the recorded addresses.

`AllCoordinatesPresent`, `GateWrapper` and `SaturationMap` combine declared protocol coordinates. Their signatures do not quantify over proposal sequences, state the accepted/correct set inclusion, count trials or implement retention. The wrapper theorem requires the semantic and operational premises above.

`Examples/LearningMachines.swift` provides illustrative type declarations. `CertifiedSelfPlay` uses Never coordinates and has no runtime learning loop. Its conformance is not evidence of a trained system's performance. The toy `LanguageModel` has a deliberately chosen non-Unique coordinate. Its failure to satisfy that particular declaration does not prove a limitation of actual language models. A pure-type witness supplies related coordinates and a derivation under its declared premises, with the relevant refusal boundary. It need not supply runtime inhabitants. An empirical performance claim needs its own operational evidence.

**AI18 (new consequences and new acquisition have different witnesses).** VC80–VC81 provide one resident chain with three distinct obligations. The present first-coordinate question and the question after S read different consequences of the same acquired a/b memory. The mixed G/S question instead requires h on the unrestricted binary family. Equal old memories with positively different mixed answers establish that requirement. A declared resident third instrument acquires h, and the resulting record supports every finite word of the admitted exchanges through existing composition.

The model therefore distinguishes an insufficient current answer, an insufficient complete accessible memory and an available new interaction. The first can be repaired by another decoder on old memory. The second needs new distinguishing evidence or an independently justified restriction of the source family. The third supplies an acquisition only when its channel, preparation and actual source relation are present. `KnowledgeClosure.swift` joins these conditions without an additional core protocol or runtime inference loop.

This is a concrete meaning of sufficient learned structure: acquired relations support a declared family of future questions. Extending that family can require additional distinctions, as VC81 shows for mixed operations. Changing the source law can instead invalidate an old transfer relation. Those are different reasons to revisit knowledge. The construction establishes reuse and necessary acquisition in its scope. It does not measure training speed or assert that every missing Swift conformance is a discovered knowledge gap.

Interference Is Substitution IiS9–IiS10 tests the scope of reuse. The two Pauli orders XZ and ZX give the same channel on every input, yet specified controlled implementations differ relative to an internal reference. IiS17's Choi matrix does not specify that extra implementation. However, IiS10's direct comparison of the two orders cancels each primitive's common phase. That comparison requires neither learning those phases nor retaining them in the learned description. Proving an answer independent of an unresolved parameter can remove an acquisition requirement. IiS12 adds a limit to that inference. IiS11–IiS13 test what acquisition can establish within a declared model. In a finite-dimensional CPTP family, exact preservation of connected, spanning pure probes forces identity without an additional assumption that the unknown channel is unitary. Approximate observations require the stated overlap and error bounds. Counting successful probes alone omits this condition.

IiS15–IiS17 connect a positive factorization, operator normalization, prepared record and actual action. The static constructors retain common sources through arithmetic, composition, acquisition and Choi assembly. Full recovery and local dephasing are different readings of the same joint record. A map can have the right Choi partial trace and fail positivity, so neither a normalization label nor an unrelated matrix supplies the whole certificate. The detailed proofs and refusal cases belong to those claims, rather than a separate learning mechanism.

A finite matrix has finitely many scalar slots. Exact finite storage also requires a representation of their values. The learner must distinguish these from identifying one exact member. The present integer constructions supply finite descriptions and source relations, without establishing a general empirical reconstruction method or a training-speed advantage.

IiS18 gives a quantum instance and an acquisition boundary. IiS19 supplies a stopping condition for this declared quantum model. Pull the requested effects backward through every admitted operation, retaining each new independent direction. Outcome-dependent continuations require the instrument branches, not only their averaged channel. This specializes VC15 and VC38: saturation ends at a proof of closure, while acquisition establishes the initial statistics to which that proof applies.

M65 supplies a route to deriving the admitted mechanism family. For two uninterpreted contents, uniform transport under all interpretations leaves four coordinate-selection operations. Recoverability leaves identity and exchange. The stronger learning task is to establish the interface's uniformity, rather than assume that a matching probe proves it. The same rule admits a useful elimination. On exactly the domain of triples with at most two distinct contents, it agrees with the fixed first-two-field swap. M65 carries that distinguishing preparation through the existing resident acquisition path. One whole already contains the local pair, a third distinct reserve content and both prepared instruments. The preparation must also be known well enough for the inference. M65 exhibits the same new response fact from F with a repeated reserve and S with a distinct reserve. If reserve contrast was not established, the resident protocol acquires its actual value and the law response together. The acquisition policy must distinguish the actual preparations too. The last outcome certifies a scope limitation rather than a law answer. In the stated six-case model, calibration followed by a response when needed achieves the optimal worst-case count of two allowed queries. Different costs or expected-cost objectives require another comparison.

This policy is an instance of VC30–VC31's existing discovery criterion. The resident acquisitions retain the source, so an observation does not destroy a still-needed distinction between candidate origins. Without that condition, even pairwise distinguishable candidates can defeat every single-specimen policy, as VC26 shows. The Cut's universal registration rule therefore supplies no shortcut from a real distinction to guaranteed access by one learner. Matter §2a states the additional scope coverage and question-preserving acquisition relations explicitly.

VC31 makes the finite success certificate independent of a search engine or separately supplied rank. A finite tree specifies one action per observed history, covers its successor beliefs, and ends where all remaining required answers agree. Its shape proves termination. Sound finite overapproximations suffice, so a canonical exact partition is unnecessary when coverage is established. The original answer labels belong to verifier descriptions, not the learner's accessible memory. `CoverDiscoverySuccessor` and `AnsweredDiscoveryNode` express the finite constructor proof, with a shared action and source-bound terminal table. An adversarial open conformance can nevertheless fake completion endpoints. `FirstDiscoveryDecision` therefore admits the existing three-row consumer by directly inspecting the actual first-action observations and original targets. It accepts a correct bare table and rejects the forged answer without trusting its proof story. Execution also binds the actual successor field, rejecting a forged selector. The decoder receives only the observation and prepared table. This finite admission replaces a trust obligation for that source pattern. General nonterminal composition and a checker for arbitrary supplied proof grammars remain open.

M32 adds a reason to preserve joint questions in learning. Even and odd three-bit parity preparations have identical marginals on every pair, but one reversible operation exposes agreement for one preparation and disagreement for the other. A model retaining all pair marginals can still omit a distinction needed by an available intervention. The missing object is a joint relation, not necessarily an additional component. Likewise a generator can return its local state after each use while producing correlated outputs. Single-output correctness does not establish a contract for independent repeated samples. Saturation must cover the later joint questions being promised.

M34 separates idempotent refinement of knowledge from a global deterministic factor of physical change. If U is surjective, E is one unchanged reading onto its image, and E∘U=F∘E at every full input, then F is surjective. If also F²=F, it must be the identity. No finite-state premise is needed. Thus an update that properly narrows candidates by a fixed fact cannot be identified with that entire contract. Its established fact, preparation and source correspondence must be retained. The same actual local value can otherwise hide phases with different next answers, as the resident reset construction proves statically. Pointwise finite stabilization with no uniform bound remains possible on an infinite domain. VI's static closure of consequences and an internally performed acquisition therefore have different inputs and obligations, without requiring an external source or a runtime solver for the closure.

VC23a makes one expressibility boundary exact. Conjoining binary Boolean constraints and hiding any number of Boolean variables cannot define ternary parity, because majority preserves every relation generated that way and breaks parity. A four-valued latent centre with three binary contacts can express it, and four states are minimal for that star shape. VC23d strengthens the carrier comparison: one three-state hidden coordinate remains insufficient with any finite Boolean hidden network, while two three-state coordinates suffice. Choosing a representation can therefore change what is expressible, even when every visible variable is binary. A learner needs a structural reason for introducing the richer carrier or higher-arity constraint. Adding more examples to an inexpressive grammar cannot substitute for that change. This particular no-go concerns the stated constraint grammar, not all neural architectures or the whole VI type language.

VC23c–VC23e turn that example into a task about the representation itself. Its inputs are a contact language Γ, established admissions P and established refusals N. Its output is either a certificate that no expression in that language can meet the evidence, or an exact separation of forced admissions, forced refusals and unresolved contacts. The obstruction applies to every permitted hidden-variable construction. The unresolved case has two compatible representations with opposite answers. It therefore supplies a reason to change the language, acquire another fact or stop claiming a determined answer. This characterizes the task mathematically. The finite type constructions below do not implement a general analyser for arbitrary supplied languages and contact records.

There is a sharp finite boundary. Against a complete binary Boolean base, every extra relation is either already expressible or makes the combined language express every finite Boolean relation. Three admitted tuples and one refused majority certify the second outcome, even with the rest of that relation unknown. A learned contact can thus change the expressive class of a medium without first being reconstructed in full. The available substitutions and all four contact facts must refer to the actual fixed relation. The generic type construction retains that table and leaves its irrelevant entries free.

Such an extension also changes which earlier deductions remain justified. More sound evidence narrows the candidate relations, while a more expressive grammar enlarges them. Cached conclusions must retain that difference in their premises. Once every Boolean relation is expressible, P and N alone leave every other contact unresolved. Further prediction must come from an established law or another justified restriction. A learner that repairs every mismatch by making its language more permissive can fit the records while losing the structural consequences it previously relied on.

These are established mathematical directions. [Giacobazzi, Ranzato and Scozzari](https://www.sci.unich.it/~scozzari/paper/JACM00.pdf) study minimal refinements and restrictions making abstract interpretations complete. [Amato and Scozzari](https://www.sci.unich.it/~amato/papers/fi11.pdf) distinguish completeness for observations from completeness at every intermediate computation. The finite expression-language test uses the preservation/definability correspondence cited in VC23c. VI's concrete task here is to retain the actual contact evidence, the allowed composition grammar and a checkable reason for each representation change. These results do not establish an exclusive capability or a new general complexity class for VI.

VI1a supplies a separate stopping criterion for acquisition and generation. If a symmetry preserves all available evidence and resources while moving the requested answer, an equivariant deterministic learner cannot recover that answer. More equivariant processing of the same complete input does not remove the obstruction. A new internal reference must reduce the surviving symmetry enough to fix the answer. For a finite symmetry group fixing the earlier input, its orbit capacity is at least the answer's orbit size. An invariant task answer may need no such reference. Parameters, training records, positional addresses and scheduling decisions count as resources when the model uses them. Their asymmetry can enable a choice, but does not by itself connect that choice to an unknown earlier event. A symmetric probability law can instead be unique while individual samples differ. This distinguishes a justified answer, an acquired reference and a newly generated decision without requiring a universal symmetry analyzer in the runtime or the type language.

VC23b makes the distinction quantitative for one finite constraint medium. Existence of a vacancy in n binary positions has an n-state central witness with binary contacts. Deterministically obtaining such a witness while preserving every position permutation requires at least 2ⁿ−1 distinguishable witness forms, and the source itself attains that bound. A cyclic orientation changes the three-position minimum from seven to four, and a total order permits three. These counts depend on the contact topology, acquisition requirement and actual context. A model's positional order is therefore a resource of the witness-producing rule. The full pattern may already supply the required witness without a separate selection mechanism or additional storage. Before constructing a generator of certificates, establish whether the consumer needs a chosen witness, the complete support pattern or only the invariant answer.

Under the declared compatibility with all injective embeddings, M65 reduces the remaining law description to equality patterns: five patterns for three inputs. Compatibility of their answers with identification of generic markers is necessary and sufficient for the stronger uniformity. This gives finite obligations for establishing the fixed-selection family, once the embedding premise is known. The pattern equations characterize the law, while the separate acquisition contract establishes evidence for its responses.

The same reduction also characterizes recovery without excluding equality inspection. M65 derives the reversible family from permutations of equality patterns and bijections of their content blocks. For triples it contains 288 laws, compared with six under the stronger interpretation condition. A proposed inverse in the same embedding-compatible language needs only one return equation per canonical pattern. Those equations imply recovery in both directions on every carrier. Thus a learner can certify an equality-sensitive reversible rule without first forcing it into the fixed-routing class. Identifying a member of this known triple family needs four prepared-input queries with complete output readings, one per pattern with at least two blocks. Binary-only preparations omit the all-distinct pattern, so repetition cannot determine that branch. The learner's preparation interface must expose the distinctions its law question requires. It still needs the language contract: matching a finite collection of physical responses alone does not establish that contract.

M65 also separates a representation's content frequencies from retained information. Its reversible four-field rule exchanges (a,a,a,b) with (a,a,b,b). Entropy of the uniformly sampled field value changes, while entropy of a complete-source ensemble is preserved. A learner using token or feature frequency entropy as a loss must therefore specify the question that this distribution describes. A change in that statistic alone does not establish loss or acquisition of the complete represented input.

That finite certificate now has a constructed consumer. After compatibility is established, the distinct-marker answer determines all five rows, so acquisition retains only this position signature. The consumer reads that fact beside arbitrary new data and binds each data-selection address to its certified marker address. Selection commutes with every payload interpretation by induction on the existing finite path grammar. Here uniformity of the consumer follows from its construction. Equal new values do not permit substituting a different path, and a different signature fails its fact input. Establishing a law's compatibility and retaining sufficient memory for its use have different requirements. Applying the guarantee to another mechanism requires the corresponding source relation.

VC83 supplies a second consequence. An actual 00→01 replacement preserves the repeated-row question through the old record even though the mechanism changes. A cross-row replacement can invalidate that reuse. Thus the appropriate transfer condition relates the question, law, data and available evidence. Separate demands to learn the entire law and the entire source can ask for distinctions the joint question does not require. The witnesses establish this reduction within the four-clause family. They do not infer that the family exhausts an unknown physical mechanism from a finite number of matching probes.

MiC3 gives another use for scoped evidence. Two independent partial repairs can jointly satisfy a task while each branch alone still fails the complete task. The useful knowledge is the certified part and its applicability relation. Under the declared independence conditions, composing those parts constructs the complete answer. MiC4 then permits reuse of preserved verdicts, including touched questions whose answers have a transport proof. A whole-task failure bit omits which partial result can already be reused.

M66 locates a related representation choice. A recorded result can have either of two sufficient supports. A fixed graph of individually necessary premises can omit that alternative entirely. `WitnessedOccurrence` retains which actual reading supplied the result, while `OccurrenceImage` can present the shared answer without that operation field. The narrower answer interface is sufficient only for questions that factor through it. Revisiting the support requires its retained address. These are static constructions and scope conditions, with no measured training-speed claim.

VC84 makes input reduction precise. Adequate subsets of a finite reading vocabulary have a unique family of inclusion-minimal members, but may have no single least member. Two correlated records can each supply the answer while their empty intersection cannot. A learner cannot delete every individually dispensable input at once. It can remove one, certify sufficiency of what remains on the admitted source domain, and continue. On a full product of independently variable coordinates, the essential coordinates do form a unique least interface. Using that conclusion for correlated data would require the missing product premise. Neither result chooses an optimal acquisition policy or proves that a changed source still satisfies the old domain relation.

M68 supplies a structural case where the necessary readings can be characterized without enumerating all answers. For balanced orientations of a fixed finite even-degree graph, a fixed set of edge readings identifies every orientation exactly when its unmeasured complement is a forest. Leaf constraints then reconstruct the rest. For one actual orientation, it suffices to meet its directed cycles, which can require fewer readings. This connects VC84's supports to an explicit source relation and decoder. IceCoverage now checks a supplied static certificate: reconstruct the original edges, preserve measured directions, exhaust their incidences, bind every local balance to those same readings, and discharge every unmeasured edge by endpoint removal. Completion of that last sequence proves the residual forest; a prefix can still strand later edges. Balance certifies that the supplied pins admit a completion, while the forest excludes another one. Applying this to a learner requires establishing the source representation and local constraints for the learner's problem.

M69 weakens the finite-whole requirement for this graph case. For the balanced even-degree family on a locally finite graph, a requested unmeasured edge is uniformly determined exactly when it is a residual bridge with a finite side. Summing the balances on that side gives a finite cut certificate from measured crossing directions. Unknown cycles inside the side or elsewhere can remain unresolved. Thus a growing or unbounded declared structure can support finite proofs for particular answers. Tests restricted to finite changes can miss alternatives differing on an infinite support, so the admitted class of differences belongs to the claim. This suggests retaining certificates by the questions and incidences they cover, with completeness checked at their actual boundary. It does not supply those graph relations for an arbitrary learned model.

**A correlated activity pattern does not identify its organizing mechanism.** [Kutch and Valero-Cuevas](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1002434) studied motor-controlled tendons in cadaveric fingers and computational muscle models. Biomechanical and task constraints produced low-dimensional coordination patterns without imposing the proposed neural grouping. Their modeled muscle responses and measured tendon contacts are different evidence. These examples show why a compact activity decomposition alone cannot identify a neural module. They do not exclude neural coordination in an intact organism.

For a learned representation, two candidate mechanisms agreeing on the observed task remain unresolved if an admitted change of support would separate their answers. Space S4 gives an exact instance: the same short–long–short body record yields displacement with alternating supports and no displacement with one support retained. An interface that omits support history cannot answer both. A useful acquisition therefore changes or reads the contact that distinguishes the candidate mechanisms. More repetitions under the old indistinguishing condition cannot supply that distinction. This is AI9's continuation requirement applied to coupled processes, not evidence that every useful learned feature must correspond to a separate physical part.

Space §3a sharpens the intervention. A changed answer after a closed projected walk rules out fixed inverse transport on a tree for that answer. It does not by itself choose between an omitted control input and an omitted state. Changing stroke speed changes a current input. To test whether a proposed present-state image omits a consequential historical distinction, compare different preparations that agree on that image and then apply the same specified probe. Different exact deterministic answers exclude one decoder from that image and probe. With stochastic predictions, individual differing outcomes are insufficient: the comparison must address the conditional laws or other asserted statistical restrictions.

VC15 then gives the retention criterion. Two preparation histories can be merged only if their resulting states agree on every admitted future question, including action availability where operations are partial. A distinguishing probe requires a finer representation. It does not locate the missing distinction in the medium rather than the controller, or prove that all earlier details remain relevant. Localizing the carrier needs an intervention that separates those explanations. For the contact model, support mode is a first candidate, followed by slip and compliance. An autonomous cycle must also account for how its controller produces that mode, as part of the same whole.

The [2026 preprint by Vega Reyes et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.20716) supplies a relevant preparation experiment: a driven granular layer's horizontal temperature departs from its matched stationary value before returning. Horizontal motion is measured experimentally, while the displayed relation to vertical temperature comes from simulations and a kinetic model. The later collapse of measured relaxation curves supports a restricted description. AC7 shows why constancy of kurtosis alone cannot certify that description's complete closure.

For VI, the concrete acquisition question is which additional present relation changes a future answer within the admitted preparations. A temperature, mean or fitted coordinate may be sufficient in one established regime and insufficient after another intervention. The drive, material and recording apparatus all remain inside the whole. Disappearance of a history effect in one retained response does not establish loss of every historical distinction, nor does retaining all history prove that any one local reader can recover it.

**A preparation procedure can retain a consequential relation that its local output omits.** [Ringbauer et al.](https://pure.hw.ac.uk/ws/files/9081981/PhysRevLett.114.090402.pdf) implement this distinction with two photonic qubits, one playing the environment. Their procedures first project onto an input labelled i and then rotate to a target labelled j. Changing i while holding j fixed can change the conditional environment entering the common later coupling. Their experimentally reconstructed processes depend on preparation. The reconstruction uses calibrated quantum operations and maximum likelihood with complete positivity imposed. That imposed property is not independently established by obtaining a positive fitted matrix.

The acquisition lesson is to retain both preparation and target until their difference has been shown irrelevant to the promised continuation. Postselection and its record belong to the preparation, so these conditional comparisons do not supply a signalling operation. This experiment does not establish identical full marginals of both participants. AC7 supplies a separate exact statement about that stronger premise: even complete separate statistics can omit a joining needed by an interaction response. On its declared finite support, alternating cycles locate the remaining ambiguity. M32 already gives a constructed common operation exposing a distinction hidden from every pair marginal.

The next useful question therefore need not be which extra scalar describes a part. It can be which joining of already identified parts changes the answer. A learner with an available distinguishing contact can acquire that relation while preserving the established local descriptions. A successful test excludes a specific merger of sources. Promising reuse under all later operations still requires VC15's closure, including availability under partial operations.

IiS24 gives a stronger attack on learning from selected successes. Local source-dependent filtering reproduces any finite conditional coincidence table, even with constant acceptance rates at every setting. Thus matching the successful answers and those rates does not establish the claimed interaction on the full source domain. Retain the rejected or missing cases and their source-setting relation, or supply a sufficient selection contract. This is VC46–VC47's availability requirement applied to acquisition itself, before treating the resulting data as evidence for a new law.

That contract is specific to the promised answer. IiS24's full-trial cover shows exactly which single-detection events defeat the constructed attack. A justified statistic can omit other records while retaining the tested restriction, including the conditions on stopping. Requiring every raw record forever would be stronger than this obligation. Likewise, a readiness record's position on a clock does not establish its independence from the settings. The acquisition proof must retain the relevant source, causal and conditional relations. It can permit memory in the apparatus when its bound is valid conditional on the retained history. A stationary independent-trial model is then unnecessary.

AC7 §6b supplies a positive completion for one such comparison. On a fixed finite support with positive weights, the two complete marginals together with alternating cycle ratios identify the whole joint law. Those ratios survive positive filtering that factors through the two declared outcome values. A change can therefore expose a missing filter input even when both observed marginals remain fixed. The explicit hidden-parity filter produces exactly that case through one participant's actual local packet. Thus the test can require a richer input description without asserting a new distant influence. Its completeness concerns this observed law, not every hidden source relation or future intervention.

M39 separates information retention from operation retention. Its last-landmark reading has no reversal map on the current labels. Its lossless overlapping-pair record permits reversal, but requires exchanging each pair's fields as well as their order. Reconstruction accuracy cannot certify that a learner's chosen transformation represents the source reversal. For one involution, pairing the current reading with its reading after reversal is the least sufficient refinement. The added coordinate must be acquired or recovered from adequate retained evidence. It cannot be supplied as the desired answer.

When that relation is established, an asymmetric observed law excludes every symmetric hidden extension preserving it. A particular test can require less: M39's response criterion needs only its pulled-back value to change sign under source reversal, with a bound for justified defects. A law must still be distinguished from a tally. Every closed recorded path has balanced arrivals and departures, so that automatic identity supplies no independent certificate of stationarity.

A fixed frame encoder commutes with frame-order reversal. Temporally derived frames and odd coordinates require their actual preceding transformation too, as M39's biological phase-field example shows. For noisy or stateful acquisition, M39 instead binds the conditional record law to the chosen source histories and bounds its symmetry defect. The inferred source law is the one with that apparatus coupled. Preservation across another preparation or removal of the apparatus requires its own relation.

Learning from the tested word preserves the order relation if the complete procedure chooses the same encoder for that word and its reverse. An order-invariant objective alone does not ensure this selected solution. Training on an unordered multiset suffices when all its choices respect that multiset. A frozen encoder from separate data avoids this adaptive dependence, while statistical use still requires the test-law assumptions conditional on its training state.

AC7 §6c identifies the additional evidence obligation. Against all reversal-invariant laws on one complete record, any level-α test has detection probability at most 2α, or α for an orientation-neutral test. A longer globally correlated record does not remove this bound. Justified separate transformations of its parts can provide a stronger test. Section 6d shows why preserving a loop's joins and individual frequencies is insufficient: its orientation can remain related to the next loop. A reversible Markov path factorization supplies equal local reversal weights even from a nonstationary preparation. More general memory preserving those same block symmetries is also allowed. The needed boundary concerns these conditional weights, not absence of all memory.

Section 6e distinguishes re-encoding, statistical comparison and intervention. Transporting the data, transformations and comparator together preserves the test. Pure changes of description give only tied invariant answers. Transformed data under a justified null law can supply comparison cases, but do not become independent observations or establish an available intervention on the referent. Include adaptive feature selection in the tested procedure or establish its invariance. Augmentation, evidence acquisition and acting on the source need their respective relations, all inside the same whole. A generated record's actual existence does not show that its asserted source history occurred.

## §8. Statement

A sound verifier can filter an arbitrary proposal stream into scoped correct outputs. Complete checking preserves correct-hit events over the same processed proposals. Coverage over retries needs a probability or fairness model, coverage within time needs a budget model, and continued access needs an implemented retaining store. None of these conditions is supplied by naming a component. This separation identifies what a VI learning mechanism must actually demonstrate.
