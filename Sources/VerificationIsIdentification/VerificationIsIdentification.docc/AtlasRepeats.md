# Which premises repeat

A claim states the premises it rests on. When one of those premises is
already carried by another, the lattice states one dependency twice: the
shorter way and the longer way to the same claim. This page prints every
such pair, read from the same parents <doc:Atlas> prints.

A repeat costs nothing at the build. It matters to a reader, who counts
the premises of a claim to see what it truly asks for. The rows below are
the places where that count is larger than the claim needs, and a page
with no rows is the lattice keeping every set minimal.

The map behind this page is <doc:Atlas>, and the premises the build needs
are in <doc:AtlasAblation>.

## The premises stated twice

No claim declares a premise another of its premises already carries.
